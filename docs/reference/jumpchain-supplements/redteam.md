# Jumpchain Supplement Stack — Redteam

> **Purpose.** Adversarial review of the recommended supplement builds. Find hidden weaknesses, exploit paths, and counter-builds that the positive-framing analyses under-emphasized.
>
> **Read first.** Read `combined-tier-list.md`, `combined-builds.md`, and `combined-synergies.md` first. This doc assumes you know what the recommended builds do.

---

## §1.0 — Build-level redteam (the "Godmode" stack as a whole)

### §1.1 — Strengths (recap)

- **Trinity of protection:** EBM Blank + Inertia of Self + Corruption Resistance = unobservable, unchangeable, uncorruptible
- **Mental immunity:** EBM Mental Resistance III + SBM Resolve 200 = flawless mind control immunity
- **Combat monster:** SBM Strength 200 + Endurance 200 + EBM Physical Prowess V = superhuman physical stats
- **CP engine:** UDS Retail Rocker + Bitch-Chan + Chain Drawback stack = ~7000 CP/jump
- **Shapeshifting:** SBM Alt-Form Shift + Independent Forms + Coalescence = full Alt-Form toolkit
- **Warehouse:** PRS Personal Reality + EBM Private Reality = integrated fortress

### §1.2 — Build-level weaknesses

**Weakness 1: The CP engine is bounded by Hiatus/Revoke math.**

Chain Drawbacks cost 3x value to Hiatus. If you have 5 Chain Drawbacks worth +1000 CP/jump, you need 3000 CP to Hiatus all of them. The "Hiatus" mechanic is supposed to let you take a break from severe drawbacks, but in practice, Hiatus costs more than the drawback's value. For a +100 drawback, Hiatus costs 300 CP — more than the 100 CP the drawback gives per jump. **You take a NET LOSS by Hiatusing.** This means the recommended builds can never take a break from their severe drawbacks. The "drawback stacking" is forever.

**Weakness 2: The trinity of protection has gaps.**

- Blank covers external observation but NOT conceptual or systemic effects (e.g., "you never existed")
- Inertia of Self covers identity/memory but NOT existence erasure (you can have Inertia + still be killed)
- Corruption Resistance covers soul but NOT physical/spiritual possession (e.g., a being that physically controls you without soul magic)
- Mental Resistance III covers mental intrusion but NOT biological mind control (e.g., pheromones that bypass your brain's higher functions)

The trinity is strong but not absolute. Setting-specific threats can bypass it.

**Weakness 3: SBM Resistance [300 CP] doesn't protect against "alteration that isn't a detrimental change."**

The SBM source says Resistance resists "detrimental changes directly caused by supernatural means." The word "detrimental" is the key. A "change" that benefits you (e.g., a setting that turns you into a deity for its own reasons) is NOT resisted. This is rarely a threat but is a real edge case.

**Weakness 4: EBM Power Toggle doesn't work on all powers.**

The EBM source says Power Toggle lets you "turn off any problematic power." But for permanent/perma-locked powers (e.g., Divinity, body refinements), Toggle may not apply. The source is ambiguous. **Verify per-perk.**

**Weakness 5: The PRS warehouse is exposed to setting-specific attacks.**

PRS gives you a Personal Reality, but the entrance is a portal. If a setting can detect/seal/follow the portal, your "fortress" is just another room. Settings with strong planar/space magic (D&D 3.5+, Elder Scrolls) can find and break into your PRS. The EBM Private Reality perk is free, but it doesn't add security — just integration.

**Weakness 6: UDS Chain Drawbacks are FOREVER (no Hiatus for many).**

The UDS source notes that some Chain Drawbacks have "No Hiatus, No Revoke" clauses. If you take Bitch-Chan, Random-Chan, or Thomas Wolfe Rule (all common), they're permanent. You can never take a break. **The CP engine is a permanent commitment, not a flexible tool.**

**Weakness 7: The Shapeshifter suite has a combat blindspot.**

SBM Independent Forms (300 CP) means "injuries don't transfer between forms." This is huge for survival but creates a combat issue: if you switch forms mid-fight, the original form's injuries are gone, but the new form's injuries are fresh. Against enemies that exploit form-switching (e.g., "I trap you in a new form"), this is a vulnerability.

**Weakness 8: The CP engine doesn't help with meta-threats.**

Retail Rocker doubles CP, but you can't BUY "immunity to Scion" with CP. The UDS drawbacks are CP sources, not power sources. Against meta-tier threats (Scion, Endbringers, Cosmic-tier entities), the CP engine is irrelevant. **You still need setting-specific perks to handle meta-threats.**

**Weakness 9: EBM Tier 3 Supernatural perks require Meteoric Mode or EP-grant perks to fund.**

The Tier 3 Supernatural perks cost -200 EP each. There are 20+ Tier 3 perks. At 200 EP starting, you can afford 1. You need either:
- Meteoric Mode (+100 EP/jump) for slow accumulation
- EP-grant perks (Lesser Essence Infusion -50 CP → 50 EP) for one-time boosts
- The GFJ body-mod house rule for free perks

**The Godmode build assumes you have Meteoric Mode + 10 jumps of EP accumulation + GFJ level 5.** If you start later or have less GFJ, you can't fund the full Tier 3 suite.

**Weakness 10: The "yuo" joke is a trap.**

The SBM Body Affinity contains an intentional typo ("yuo" instead of "you"). The Notes section confirms it's intentional. Some players have tried to "fix" it and accidentally invalidated their pick. **Read the Notes section carefully.**

## §2.0 — Per-perk redteam (every major element)

### §2.1 — Retail Rocker (UDS)

**Text:** "Discounts are for poor people. You're a Jumper of Quality. You always pay full price. Always. But in exchange, you now get double the CP from all drawbacks you take. ... You get +100 JP per jump for Slot-O-Matic."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Always pay full price" is severe.** Without Retail Rocker, you can take Drawbacks that don't cost you (free perks that grant CP). With Retail Rocker, every free Drawback becomes costly. **Retail Rocker is incompatible with most "free Drawback" synergies.**
2. **The +100 JP is small.** Slot-O-Matic is a niche perk. The +100 JP doesn't offset the loss of discounts.
3. **The "double CP" doesn't apply to Charms/Boon-granted CP.** The source says "all drawbacks you take." If you receive CP from a non-Drawback source (e.g., jump-native Origin bonus), Retail Rocker doesn't double it.

**Counter-counter:** Pair with Gauntlet-Kun (which doubles ALL UDS Drawbacks). Gauntlet-Kun + Retail Rocker means UDS drawbacks are doubled twice (multiplicative). Total: x4 UDS drawback CP.

### §2.2 — Gauntlet-Kun (UDS)

**Text:** "All your Universal Drawbacks are worth twice as much… but you start every jump with Zero CP and must get all your CP from Drawbacks. ... He won't send you home. Instead, he'll take all the perks and items you got from any jump you fail and hold onto them until you Spark."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Start every jump with Zero CP."** This is brutal. If your Chain Drawbacks are worth <1000 CP/jump, you're net negative. You NEED 1000+ CP of Chain Drawbacks to break even.
2. **"Lose perks from failed jumps."** This is the killer. If you chain-fail, you lose all the perks you've built up. The Jumper becomes a hollow shell of your former self.
3. **No Hiatus, No Revoke.** Permanent commitment.
4. **"All CP must come from Drawbacks."** You can't use Companion CP, jump-native Origin CP, or other CP sources. Only Drawbacks.
5. **Doesn't double Retail Rocker.** The source explicitly states this.

**Counter-counter:** Take Retail Rocker + Gauntlet-Kun together. Retail Rocker doubles CP from drawbacks (which Gauntlet-Kun also doubles). Total: x4 CP. But you still start at 0.

### §2.3 — Bitch-Chan (UDS)

**Text:** "She insists you always take a full slate of native Drawbacks in every jump. If there isn't a limit, this means at least 600 CP worth of drawbacks."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"At least 600 CP" is a floor, not a ceiling.** If the jump has Drawbacks worth 1000+ CP, you take those. The CP engine scales with the jump, not against it.
2. **"Harder to cheese."** The drawbacks become mandatory engagement, not optional flavor. You can't avoid them.
3. **Chain Only, No Hiatus, No Revoke.** Permanent.

**Counter-counter:** Take Sadistic Bitch-Chan (which doubles jump-specific Drawback value). Combined: you take high-value Drawbacks AND double their CP. Total: 1200-2000 CP/jump from native Drawbacks alone.

### §2.4 — Random-Chan (UDS)

**Text:** "You (as either the jumper or author) cannot dictate the order of your jumps. You, the Author, must generate a jump list (using at least 250 different jumps)."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"250 different jumps" is a LOT.** You need a list of 250+ jumps. For niche settings (SCP, individual CYOAs), this is impractical.
2. **"Pick 1 of 2 random" is the escape clause.** You can re-roll, but you lock out the unchosen one. This prevents true randomness.
3. **"No Revoke."** Permanent. Can't undo.

**Counter-counter:** Take Pseudo-Random-Chan (you control, +50 CP). For most players, Pseudo-Random-Chan is the better deal (cheaper, same CP).

### §2.5 — Thomas Wolfe Rule (UDS)

**Text:** "You can't ever go home again. You can never take the Go Home option."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **Time-limited.** Only +100 CP for 8 jumps, then +50 for 8 more. After 16 jumps, no CP. **The drawback's CP engine exhausts.**
2. **"Chain Only, No Hiatus, No Revoke."** Permanent commitment.
3. **"If you chainfail, you go to a non-native reality."** If you fail, you're stuck in a random world. Not home.

**Counter-counter:** Combine with River Corollary (+100 CP) for total "no home, no return" = +200 CP for 8 jumps.

### §2.6 — EBM Blank I-II

**Text:** "Abilities that gather information about your past, present, or future do not work on you. This includes scrying, divination, mind reading, and even cold reading and lie detection."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **Doesn't cover "observation without supernatural means."** A camera watching you is not affected. A mundane spy network is not affected.
2. **Tier I leaves "mundane skills" partially functional.** "Purely mundane skills at cold reading or lie detection will be less effective but still function to some degree." Tier I doesn't fully block.
3. **Tier II's "false information" trick is limited.** You feed them "the type of info they expect." If they expect something specific (e.g., a particular number), you have to match it.

**Counter-counter:** Combine with Inertia of Self (identity protection) and Corruption Resistance (soul protection). Plus, in PRS, you can "hide" from observation by being in your warehouse.

### §2.7 — EBM Inertia of Self I-II

**Text:** Tier I: "You resist any attempt to alter your history, identity, mind, or soul, or any other aspect of your fundamental being." Tier II: Adds "You cannot be copied, replicated, or simulated without your consent."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **Doesn't protect against erasure.** "Resist alteration" is not "survive erasure." If a being kills you by erasing you from history (not altering, erasing), Inertia doesn't help.
2. **"Other aspects of your fundamental being" is vague.** Does it cover your body's structure? Your Patron's blessing? Your Companion's bond? Unclear.
3. **Tier II's "without your consent" has gaps.** If you consent (e.g., under Master effects), it works. Mental Resistance III blocks Master, so this is moot.

**Counter-counter:** Combine with AEIOU (no clones, no mirror selves) for total identity protection.

### §2.8 — EBM Mental Resistance III

**Text:** "You are immune to all forms of mental intrusion, control, or manipulation. This includes telepathy, mind control, emotional manipulation, memory alteration, and even passive observation of your thoughts."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"All forms" is absolute. Setting-specific overrides may apply.** Some settings have "absolute mind control" that fiat-overrides perks. The EBM text doesn't specify how this interacts with setting fiat.
2. **Doesn't cover biological/neurological control.** If a being controls your brain chemistry directly (not via mental magic), Mental Resistance doesn't apply.
3. **No "offensive" capability.** Mental Resistance III is purely defensive. You can't use it to project mind control.

**Counter-counter:** Combine with SBM Resolve 200 (resists supernatural control, dispels at jump end). Double-layer protection.

### §2.9 — SBM Resistance [300 CP]

**Text:** "Your form resists detrimental changes directly caused by supernatural means. The more detrimental an effect would be, the easier it is to resist them."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Detrimental" is subjective.** A being that "improves" you isn't resisted. This is rarely a threat but is a real edge case.
2. **"More detrimental = easier to resist" is asymmetric.** A sleep spell is hard to resist (low detriment). A soul-rend is easy to resist (high detriment). This means weak attacks sometimes slip through.
3. **No offense. Resistance is pure defense.**

**Counter-counter:** Combine with EBM Personal Immunity (immune to your own powers' side effects). The combination covers self and external supernatural effects.

### §2.10 — SBM Independent Forms [300 CP]

**Text:** "Any Alt-Forms you possess deteriorate independently of each other, meaning that age, starvation, sickness and injuries will not transfer over when changing forms."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"You count as being in every single one of them" (with Coalescence).** If you have 5 Alt-Forms and Coalesce them, you have 5 separate bodies. Damage to one is damage to all.
2. **"Retire an Alt-Form that has reached senescence."** If an Alt-Form ages out, you lose it. Time pressure.
3. **"Replace it with a new, youthful form" requires SBM Aligned Form Selection.** Not specified in the source — verify in-game.

**Counter-counter:** Use EBM Ageless II (no aging) on your primary form. Other forms age out, but your primary doesn't.

### §2.11 — EBM Divinity

**Text:** "You become a god. Choose a domain."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Domain" is undefined.** The EBM source is vague on what a "domain" entails. Setting-specific.
2. **Divine-tier perks have setting-specific effects.** Some settings make "god" weak (Worm, low-power urban fantasy). Others make it strong (D&D, high-power mythology).
3. **May attract setting-specific attention.** Becoming a god in a setting with jealous gods (D&D Forgotten Realms, Warhammer) creates problems.

**Counter-counter:** Pick a domain that's contextually low-key (e.g., "the god of small forgotten things"). Less attention.

### §2.12 — PRS Personal Reality

**Text:** "You gain a pocket dimension that you can customize."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Customize" is constrained by WP.** You have 100 WP default. Big projects (300 WP fortress) require WP-grant sources.
2. **The entrance is a portal.** Settings with portal-detection can find you.
3. **No automatic defense.** Your warehouse can be invaded unless you add defenses (which cost WP).
4. **Companions can enter.** If a Companion betrays you, they have access to your warehouse.

**Counter-counter:** EBM Private Reality (free) integrates the warehouse into your body, so it's harder to access externally. Plus, take EBM Reflexive Reinforcement I-II (dual HP bars) for combat inside the warehouse.

### §2.13 — EBM Private Reality

**Text:** "Integrate any property you own into your Personal Reality."

**Weaknesses:**
1. **"Property" is undefined.** Does it cover your car? Your house? Your home country? Unclear.
2. **The integration is one-way.** You can't extract the property back out without potentially breaking the warehouse.
3. **The property is now in your warehouse.** If the warehouse is destroyed, the property is destroyed.

**Counter-counter:** This perk is free and the integration is the only way to get a "permanent" property. The trade is acceptable.

## §3.0 — Synergy redteam (where the recommended combinations don't combine well)

### §3.1 — EBM Tier 3 Supernatural + SBM Tier 3 Augment cost-overlap

**Claimed synergy:** Both EBM and SBM give you Tier 3 superhuman abilities. Stack them.

**Reality:** EBM Tier 3 Supernatural costs -200 EP. SBM Tier 3 Augment costs -200 CP (or -50 with discount). You can afford 1 of each per jump. The total cost is ~250 EP + ~100 CP per Tier 3 perk. Over 10 Tier 3 perks, you need 2500 EP + 1000 CP. The CP is fine (UDS engine), but the EP requires Meteoric Mode + 10 jumps of accumulation.

**The synergy is real, but slow.** Don't expect to fund the full Tier 3 suite in your first jump.

### §3.2 — UDS Drawback Stack + Aeioetic (AEIOU) interaction

**Claimed synergy:** AEIOU is "no clones, no mirror selves." UDS doesn't directly address cloning. No conflict.

**Reality:** AEIOU is a bedrock rule that "cannot be Hiatused, cannot be Revoked, cannot be touched by a Gauntlet." This means AEIOU persists even when you take Gauntlet-Kun (which is supposed to be hardcore mode). AEIOU is the one UDS drawback that even Gauntlet-Kun doesn't make harder.

**The issue:** AEIOU's "no cloning" prevents some jump-native perks (e.g., Naruto Shadow Clone, Multiple Man) from working. If you take AEIOU, you can't use clone perks. **Verify each jump's perks for clone-grants.**

### §3.3 — EBM Power Toggle + EBM Tier 3 Supernatural capstone

**Claimed synergy:** Power Toggle (-50 EP) lets you turn off any power. Combined with Tier 3 Supernatural capstones, you can disable problematic abilities.

**Reality:** Power Toggle "lets you turn off any power." But for abilities that are integral to your body (e.g., Regeneration III, Mental Resistance III), Toggle may not apply. The source is ambiguous. **Verify per-perk whether Toggle works.**

### §3.4 — SBM Strength 200 + EBM Physical Prowess V

**Claimed synergy:** Both give you superhuman strength. Stack them.

**Reality:** They might not stack additively. SBM Strength 200 is "comic-book peak human" (2 tons). EBM Physical Prowess V is "x100 peak human" (200 tons). If they stack, you have 202 tons. If they don't stack, the higher one wins (200 tons). The source is silent on cross-supplement stacking.

**The conservative read:** Take only one. The higher tier (EBM) is more cost-effective. SBM Strength 200 is for builds that skip EBM.

### §3.5 — PRS + EBM Private Reality integration

**Claimed synergy:** EBM Private Reality integrates property into PRS. They're the same space.

**Reality:** PRS and EBM Private Reality are designed to integrate, but the source text doesn't explicitly say "they merge." It's a community interpretation. **Some GMs may rule that PRS is a "Personal Reality" and EBM Private Reality is a separate "property integration," and they coexist but don't merge.**

## §4.0 — Counter-builds (what beats the recommended builds)

### §4.1 — "The Anti-Drawback" build

**Strategy:** Take only free UDS drawbacks (Safety Dance, AEIOU). Skip the UDS CP engine entirely. Use the EP/CP savings for more Body Mod perks.

**Strengths:**
- No severe drawbacks
- More Body Mod budget
- Cleaner build

**Weaknesses:**
- No CP engine (~1000 CP/jump base only)
- No Retail Rocker doubling
- No Gauntlet-Kun

**When it beats the recommended builds:** When the CP engine isn't needed (early chain, low-power jumps). The trade is quality of life for raw CP.

### §4.2 — "The Body Mod Specialist" build

**Strategy:** Take only SBM (skip EBM). Use the 1000 CP for full SBM optimization. Skip the EBM Mode system entirely.

**Strengths:**
- Cleaner math (no Mode complexity)
- All SBM Augments maxed
- Comic-book scaling

**Weaknesses:**
- No EBM Tier 3 Supernatural capstones
- No Divinity
- No 18 Essences
- No Private Reality

**When it beats the recommended builds:** When you want a focused shapeshifter or stat-block build without the complexity.

### §4.3 — "The Companion Pump" build

**Strategy:** Take No Drawbacks for You!-San + And Only You! + Bitch-Chan. Pump all CP to companions. Be a support Jumper.

**Strengths:**
- Companions are god-tier
- You don't need Body Mod investment (companions carry)
- Lower personal CP commitment

**Weaknesses:**
- You're personally weaker (no Tier 3 Supernatural capstones)
- Companions can betray you
- Lose agency

**When it beats the recommended builds:** When the chain has multiple Jumpers and you want to be the supply chain.

### §4.4 — "The Single Essence Specialist" build

**Strategy:** Take EBM with Single Essence Mode + one Essence (e.g., Warlord). Max that Essence's perks. Skip the multi-Essence Mode.

**Strengths:**
- Focused build
- Cheaper than Multi Essence
- Strong identity

**Weaknesses:**
- Less flexible
- No cross-Essence perks

**When it beats the recommended builds:** When you want a clear archetype (Warlord, Mage, etc.) without spread.

### §4.5 — "The GFJ Specialist" build

**Strategy:** Take GFJ to level 10, take ALL the body-mod house rule perks. Skip UDS, skip EBM, skip SBM.

**Strengths:**
- Massive body mod budget
- All GFJ perks
- No UDS drawbacks

**Weaknesses:**
- No Tier 3 Supernatural capstones
- No PRS warehouse
- Limited to GFJ's perk list

**When it beats the recommended builds:** When you love the GFJ perk list and want to use it as your body mod.

## §5.0 — The single biggest stack weakness

**Of all the recommended build weaknesses, the most dangerous is:**

**§1.2 Weakness 9: Tier 3 Supernatural funding requires Meteoric Mode + 10 jumps + GFJ level 5.**

If you can't fund the Tier 3 Supernatural suite, the "Godmode" build degrades to a "Strong Build." The S-tier perks (Personal Immunity, Power Drain Protection, Cheat Death, Master of Many Arts III, Defense Piercing Techniques II) are the difference between "very strong" and "godmode." Without them, you're just a well-built Jumper.

**The fix:** Take Meteoric Mode (100 EP/jump) + GFJ level 5+ (100+ EP from body-mod house rule) + EP-grant perks (Lesser Essence Infusion -50 CP → 50 EP). Total: ~200-300 EP/jump from UDS + GFJ. Over 10 jumps, you can fund 10-15 Tier 3 Supernatural perks.

**The second-most dangerous is:**

**§1.2 Weakness 6: UDS Chain Drawbacks are forever.**

The CP engine is a permanent commitment. If you regret your drawback stack, you can't undo it. **Choose your Chain Drawbacks carefully.** The 3x Hiatus cost and the "No Revoke" clauses on Bitch-Chan, Random-Chan, Thomas Wolfe Rule, etc. mean these are forever.

**The third:**

**§1.2 Weakness 1: Hiatus math is a net loss.**

The Hiatus mechanic is supposed to let you take a break. In practice, it costs more than the drawback gives. **The recommended builds can never take a break from their severe drawbacks.**

## §6.0 — Recommendations based on redteam

1. **Verify the Tier 3 Supernatural funding math in-game.** If you can't fund the full suite, drop Master of Many Arts III (the most niche) first.

2. **Consider Anti-Synergy 4 (Mental Resistance III vs Master/Stranger builds).** If you want to USE mental powers, take Mental Resistance II (strong resistance) not III (flawless immunity).

3. **Take Singular Form (SBM) over Alt-Form Shift if you don't need shapeshifting.** Singular Form is free and pre-empts forced-form issues. Alt-Form Shift costs nothing but requires more complex maintenance.

4. **Don't take Bitch-Chan + Random-Chan + Thomas Wolfe Rule + River Corollary all together unless you want maximum CP.** The combined drawback load is severe: 4 eyes impaired, 160% size, can't go home, can't return, personality reset.

5. **Consider taking Pseudo-Random-Chan instead of Random-Chan.** Pseudo-Random-Chan is +50 CP (vs +200) and you control the jump order. The "loss" of jump randomness is minimal for a min-maxer.

6. **Take AEIOU only if you don't need clone perks.** Naruto Shadow Clone, Multiple Man, and similar clone-based abilities are blocked by AEIOU. **Verify each jump's perk list.**

7. **Test PRS security in-game.** Your warehouse is exposed to portal-detection. Take EBM Private Reality + SBM Resistance 300 for layered security.

8. **Don't assume cross-supplement stacking is additive.** EBM Tier 3 + SBM Tier 3 may not stack. Take one or the other for each perk, not both.

## §7.0 — Verdict on the recommended builds

**Build 5: Godmode (no compromises)** is strong but has hidden vulnerabilities. The UDS CP engine is dominant, the Body Mod suite is comprehensive, and the PRS warehouse is integrated. But the Tier 3 Supernatural funding math is tight, the Chain Drawback commitment is forever, and the cross-supplement stacking is uncertain.

**Strengths (unchanged):**
- ~7000 CP/jump from UDS engine
- Trinity of protection
- Offense-defense loop
- Shapeshifting toolkit
- Personal Reality fortress

**Weaknesses (newly identified):**
- Tier 3 Supernatural funding requires Meteoric + GFJ + EP-grant perks
- UDS Chain Drawbacks are forever
- Hiatus math is a net loss
- Trinity of protection has gaps (conceptual, biological, systemic)
- Cross-supplement stacking is uncertain
- PRS warehouse is exposed to portal-detection
- Some combinations are speculative (Hate Squad escalation, Divine domains)

**For a player who wants the Godmode build:** Take it. The strengths outweigh the weaknesses. The ambiguities (cross-supplement stacking, Divine domains, PRS security) are edge cases you can adjudicate at the table.

**For a player who wants a tighter build:** Take Build 2 (Combat Monster). It has the best ratio of perks-per-CP and the most S-tier perks without the severe UDS drawback load. The Quality of Life is low but the perk suite is solid.

**For a player who wants the cleanest build:** Take Build 4 (Self-Sufficient Survivor). It teaches you the supplement structure without overwhelming you. Layer in combat or social as you learn.

## §8.0 — What I missed before this redteam

The original combined docs were positive-framing. The redteam found:

1. **Hiatus math is a net loss** — not addressed in any prior doc.
2. **The "yuo" joke is a real trap** — players have accidentally invalidated picks.
3. **Cross-supplement stacking is uncertain** — the sources don't explicitly say EBM + SBM Tier 3 stack.
4. **Trinity of protection has gaps** — conceptual, biological, systemic threats can bypass.
5. **PRS warehouse is exposed** — settings with portal-detection can find you.
6. **Tier 3 Supernatural funding is tight** — requires Meteoric + GFJ + EP-grant.
7. **AEIOU blocks clone perks** — Naruto Shadow Clone, Multiple Man, etc. are blocked.
8. **UDS Chain Drawbacks are forever** — Hiatus is a net loss, Revoke is barred on most.
9. **Bitch-Chan + Sadist-Chan conflict** — must take Sadistic Bitch-Chan (combined) or neither.
10. **SBM Resistance 300 doesn't protect against improvement** — only "detrimental" changes.

The redteam exposes the assumptions I made. The Godmode build is strong but not invincible.
