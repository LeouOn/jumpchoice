# Jumpchain Supplement Stack — Sample Builds

> **Purpose.** Concrete, budget-balanced sample builds showing how the 5 supplements compose. Each build has a theme, a perk list, and a per-jump CP projection.
>
> **Read first.** Read `combined-tier-list.md` for the top picks. Read each per-supplement analysis for full perk details. These builds assume you have access to the full stack (EBM + SBM + PRS + UDS + GFJ).

---

## §0. How the supplements layer

A standard Jumper can take all 5 supplements. The order matters for some interactions but not others. The canonical order:

1. **Universal Drawbacks Supplement (UDS)** — taken first, establishes Chain Drawback baseline
2. **Body Mod** — either EBMS (with Mode system) or SBM (with Affinity) or BOTH
3. **Personal Reality Supplement (PRS)** — taken after Body Mod, integrates with your body
4. **Generic First Jump (GFJ)** — taken as the first jump, bootstraps the chain

**Budget assumption for these builds:** 1000 CP starting in a normal jump (after UDS Chain Drawbacks applied). +600 to +3000 CP from UDS depending on build.

## §1. Build 1: "The Maximum Drawback Stacker" (CP engine)

**Theme.** Maximum CP per jump. Goal: so much CP that you can buy anything in any jump. Trades quality of life for raw power.

**Supplements taken:** UDS (full), EBM (no Mode complexity, just perks), PRS (small), GFJ (level 1, for the +100 EP starting boost)

### UDS Chain Drawback Stack

| Drawback | CP/jump | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Retail Rocker | Special | Doubles all drawback CP, +100 JP/jump |
| Bitch-Chan | +200 | Forces 600 CP native drawbacks, harder to cheese |
| Random-Chan | +200 | No Revoke. Author-controlled jump list (you're the author). |
| Pseudo-Random-Chan | — | Conflicts with Random-Chan (Variant rule). Skip. |
| Thomas Wolfe Rule | +100 | Can't go home. Time-limited. |
| River Corollary | +100 | Can't return to past jumps. |
| Refresher Course | +100 | Personality reset. |
| Old Home Week | +100 | Warehouse-home connection. |
| Cyclopes Psi-Ops | +50 | One eye. Cheap. |
| A Giant Adventure! | +50 | 160% size. Cheap. |
| All the Hats | +50 | Gag. Cheap comedy. |
| **Subtotal** | **+950/jump** | |

Plus Bitch-Chan forces 600 CP of native drawbacks per jump, doubled to 1200 by Retail Rocker.

**Total effective CP/jump: ~3100.**

Over 10 jumps: **~31,000 CP.** Over 50 jumps: **~155,000 CP.** This is a CP engine that scales to absurdity.

### EBM Build (Mundane Start Mode, No Essence)

To stay under the UDS "must limit you" clause (the drawbacks limit you severely: blind, big, weird, personality-resetting, can't go home), the EBM build focuses on utility, not combat.

| Pick | Cost | Why |

| No Access | 0 | Can't buy EP through perks (forces careful planning) |

| Ageless II | -100 EP | Don't age. |

| **Total EP spent** | **-650 EP** | (Well under 500 starting EP, so this requires Meteoric or similar advancement to fund) |

### SBM Build (No — taking EBM only for this build to save CP)

Skipped. The CP engine is the point of this build; not a single-supplement min-max.

### PRS Build (Small)

Just enough to have a Personal Reality. Take only:
- Personal Reality facilities as needed (most comes from the 100 WP default)
- Don't spend WP on big structures; use it for utility

### GFJ Build (Level 1 only)

Take level 1 perks to bootstrap. The body-mod house rule is the key — it adds perks from GFJ to your EBM/SBM body mod.

### Per-Jump CP Projection

| Source | CP |
|--------|-----|
| Base jump CP | 1000 |
| UDS Chain Drawbacks (doubled) | +1900 |
| Bitch-Chan forced native (doubled) | +1200 |
| UDS Single Jump (All the Hats, etc.) | +50 |
| **Total per jump** | **~4150** |

### Strengths
- Unbounded CP. Can buy anything.
- UDS drawbacks force you to engage with the setting (Bitch-Chan makes drawbacks "harder to cheese").
- Retail Rocker scales multiplicatively.

### Weaknesses
- All the Drawbacks stack: one eye, 160% size, weird, warehouse-home connection, no going home, can't return, personality reset, blind to one eye, can't speak (none here but the option is there).
- EBM Mundane Start Mode caps your EP at 500 starting + 250/jump from UDS. Not enough for Tier 3 Supernatural capstones.
- PRS is small (you spent your CP on the engine, not the warehouse).

### Verdict
**The CP engine build.** If your goal is to become the most CP-rich Jumper in the chain, this is the build. The drawbacks are severe but manageable if you don't care about quality of life.

### PRS Build (Medium)

Use WP to build a fortress:

### GFJ Build (Level 5, body-mod house rule)

Take level 1-5 perks. Each level adds to your body mod budget. The body-mod house rule means all GFJ perks integrate into your EBM/SBM body.

### Per-Jump CP Projection

| Source | CP |
|--------|-----|
| Base jump CP | 1000 |
| UDS Chain Drawbacks (doubled) | +1300 |
| **Total per jump** | **~2300** |

Plus 200 starting EP, scaling +100/level.

### Strengths
- S-tier Supernatural perks. You can solo most settings.
- Body Mod dual-stack (EBM + SBM) gives you both stat-block precision and comic-book scaling.
- Blank + Inertia + Corruption Resistance = trinity of protection.

### Weaknesses
- EP budget is tight. The 2050 EP spend requires the EP-grant perks or GFJ scaling. If you can't fund, drop Defense Piercing Techniques II or Heightened Reactions IV.
- 200 CP from Gunshy is "single jump" — you have to retake it each jump.
- Retail Rocker forces full price. The 200 CP tier-2 SBBM Augments cost 50 CP even with discounts, so the loss of normal 50% discount matters for the 100 CP tier Augments from non-Body Affinity builds (you'd have to use SBM's free tier).

### Verdict
**The combat monster build.** If your goal is to solo a setting's combat, this build has the S-tier Supernatural perks for it. The CP engine is moderate, not maximum.

## §7. Choosing your build

**If you're new to the stack:** Start with Build 4 (Self-Sufficient). It teaches you the supplement structure without overwhelming you. Then layer in combat or social as you learn.

**If you want to min-max:** Build 2 (Combat Monster). It has the best ratio of perks-per-CP and the most S-tier Supernatural perks.

**If you want to break the game:** Build 5 (Godmode). The CP engine is unbeatable, and the perk suite covers every threat.

**If you want to play a character:** Build 3 (Social God). It's the most "roleplay-friendly" because it has the mind-control immunity and social skills without the severe drawbacks.

**If you want to be the CP engine for your chain:** Build 1 (Maximum Drawback Stacker). You're the supply chain for the whole group.
