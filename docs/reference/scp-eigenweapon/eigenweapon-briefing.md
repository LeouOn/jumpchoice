## What is Eigenweapon V3.0?

A post-apocalyptic SCP-universe CYOA by aromage (u/Thearomage). Released March 2023. The setting is nobledark-to-nobelbright: the apocalypse (K-Day) happened, the Foundation collapsed, but humanity is rebuilding. You play a survivor building a new Guardian organization.

**Format:** Image-only (5 pages, heavily stylized). No interactive HTML version exists.

**Community rating:** Voted best SCP CYOA on r/makeyourchoice.

---

## How to play (structure)

1. **Earliest Memories** — Pick your origin (how you survived K-Day)
2. **Guardian Faction** — Pick which surviving power you serve
3. **Nation** — Pick your home city / safe haven
4. **Major Projects** — Pick your primary power source(s)
5. **Minor Projects** — Pick your secondary abilities
6. **Drawbacks** — Take penalties for bonus picks
7. **Allies** — Pick your companions
8. **Lesser Conflicts** — Pick local threats to engage
9. **Greater Conflicts** — Pick existential threats to face
10. **Guardians + Purpose** — Define your organization's scale and mission

> **Note:** Exact pick counts are [UNVERIFIED]. Community posts suggest 2 Major, 4 Minor, 4 Allies, 4 Lesser, 3 Greater — but the CYOA images are authoritative.
