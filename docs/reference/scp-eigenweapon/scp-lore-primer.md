# SCP Lore Primer for Eigenweapon V3.0

> The SCP concepts you need to understand before playing Eigenweapon CYOA V3.0. Organized by priority. This primer covers SCP wiki canon; the CYOA may have its own version of some lore elements.

---

## Tier 1 — Must understand

### The Foundation (SCP Foundation)
"Secure, Contain, Protect." The organization that contained anomalous objects, entities, and locations to protect humanity. In Eigenweapon: the Foundation collapsed during K-Day. The Foundation faction is the remnant trying to rebuild.

Key structures: Sites (containment facilities, Site-19 is most famous), MTFs (Mobile Task Forces for field ops), O5 Council (13 overseers), D-Class (expendable test subjects).

### Object Classes
| Class | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Safe | Reliably contained |
| Euclid | Unpredictable, needs active containment |
| Keter | Extremely hostile, very hard to contain |
| Thaumiel | Used by Foundation to contain other SCPs |
| Apollyon | Impossible to contain, inevitable destruction |

### K-Class Scenarios
The Foundation's classification for apocalypse events. XK-Class = End of the World. ZK-Class = Reality Failure. In Eigenweapon, K-Day was a K-Class (or worse) event.

### The Veil Protocol
The Foundation's policy of keeping anomalies secret from the public. Post-K-Day: the Veil is broken. Everyone knows anomalies are real.

## Tier 2 — Should understand

### Sarkicism
A religion based on flesh manipulation and biological horror. Worships cosmic entities (Grand Karcist Ion, Yaldabaoth). Two branches: Proto-Sarkic (rural, primitive) and Neo-Sarkic (urban, corporate). Fundamentally opposed to the Church of the Broken God (flesh vs machine).

### The Church of the Broken God (Mekhanite)
Worships Mekhane, a mechanical deity who was shattered. Goal: reassemble Mekhane by finding fragments. Practice mechanical augmentation. Leader: Robert Bumaro. Three sects: Original, Broken Church, Maxwellism.

### The Serpent's Hand
A loose organization of anomalous individuals who oppose containment. They believe anomalies should be free. Have access to the Wanderer's Library (a multiversal library accessed through dimensional "Ways").

### The GOC (Global Occult Coalition)
UN-backed organization that destroys anomalies (vs Foundation's containment). "Kill-on-sight" policy. Better funded, more politically connected, more aggressive.

### Marshall, Carter & Dark (MC&D)
A British enterprise that sells anomalous items/services to the ultra-wealthy. Capitalists, not heroes or villains.

### Memetics, Cognitohazards, Infohazards, Antimemes
- **Memetic**: ideas that spread like viruses through perception
- **Cognitohazard**: harms you when you PERCEIVE it (see, hear, read)
- **Infohazard**: harms you when you KNOW about it
- **Antimeme**: erases your ability to perceive/remember it

### Amnestics
Memory-wiping drugs used by the Foundation. Class A through E, different strengths. Post-K-Day: the amnestic infrastructure collapsed. People remember.

### Scranton Reality Anchors (SRAs)
Devices that suppress reality-warping in a local area. The Foundation used them to contain reality benders. Post-K-Day: SRA infrastructure collapsed. Reality benders are unconstrained.

### The Wanderer's Library
The Serpent's Hand's base. A multiversal library accessible through "Ways" (dimensional shortcuts). Contains knowledge from across the multiverse. Run by non-human Librarians.

### Thaumic Energy / Akiva Radiation
- **Thaumic energy**: the SCP-universe magic/anomalous physics energy
- **Akiva radiation**: stimulates magical powers (referenced in Eigenweapon's Project Caesar)

## Tier 3 — Specific SCPs referenced in Eigenweapon

### SCP-1000 (Bigfoot / The Solidarity)
Bigfoot is real and was the dominant species before humans. In Eigenweapon: SCP-1000 is the "Solidarity" — a Foundation weapon.

### Pattern Screamers
Consciousnesses embedded in reality itself. Their existence is paradoxical. Their screams distort reality. A Greater Conflict in Eigenweapon.

### The Hanged King
A reality-bending cosmic entity associated with Alagadda (the underworld). Reentered baseline reality when seals to his city weakened. A Greater Conflict in Eigenweapon.

### SCP-3688 ("Safety Dance")
A kinetoglyph-based magic system. Referenced in Eigenweapon's Project Hermes.

### Wanderer's Library "Ways"
Dimensional shortcuts between locations. Referenced in Eigenweapon's Project Janus. aromage confirmed these are canonical to the broader shared universe.

## Concepts unique to Eigenweapon's setting

### K-Day
The apocalypse event that ended the old world. The exact cause is [UNVERIFIED from community sources — the CYOA text is authoritative]. Result: Foundation collapsed, billions died, world population ~182 million by 2082.

### Eigenweapons
Cosmic-tier anomalous weapons built by various factions. In V3.0, the named Titans are: The Soldier, The Golem, The Lich, The Terror, The Horror, The Thinker. Each was built to fight cosmic threats. They failed collectively (K-Day happened anyway).

### Lif
The AI every player starts with. Bonded on technological, thaumological, and psychic levels. Project Singularity upgrades Lif to a much more powerful form.

### The Guardian Factions
The surviving powers post-K-Day. They control territory, resources, and anomalous knowledge. You join one (or build your own). The factions correspond to SCP canon Groups of Interest (GOIs).

### Nations / Safe Havens
Surviving cities where civilization persists. Each has a different culture, security level, and relationship with anomalies. Three Portlands and the Wanderer's Library are the most well-known.
