# Worm CYOA V6.1 — Anti-Trump Stack Analysis (v2 — Repaired)

> **Repaired 2026-06-20.** v1's conclusion ("there is no hard counter to a fully-built Trump-stack") was WRONG. The perk **Trumped the Trump** (Perks and Drawbacks, 3 SP) literally says *"Unless you will it to be otherwise, Trump powers do not work on you."* This v2 corrects the conclusion and adds the missing hard-counter build.

A Trump-stack (FQ + Oberon + Eidolon + PM) is the strongest single build. What beats it?

## Threat inventory

Threats to a Trump-stack:

### 1. Pre-emptive kill
- **Mover speed + Blaster range** — kill the Trumper before they engage
- **Sniper setup** — one shot, no chance to use touch powers
- **Lung-style regen** (Power Copy, 9 SP) — survive the first hit, return fire
- **Eidolon (random powers)** — RNG might give him speed/blaster
- **Verdict**: **WEAK counter**. A FQ-stack is too fast and has too many utility powers to be pre-emptively killed.

### 2. Master effects
- **Cherish, Regent, Imp** — control them before they touch you. Note: Khepri (Tier 3, 10m Master) is also viable but requires getting close.
- **Master-resistant individuals** — can break Master effects
- **Verdict**: **STRONG counter** IF you have Master-resist. The Trumper needs to be a Master, not a Trump, to be useful.

### 3. Stranger effects
- **Imp (Power Copy, 11 SP)** — untraceable
- **Nice Guy (Power Copy, 12 SP)** — universal "I'm a nice guy" aura
- **Verdict**: **STRONG counter**. If they can't touch you, FQ/Oberon don't work. Eidolon and PM don't either.

### 4. Precog immunity
- **Suicide/Det Mode** — *you* are the Trumper, you can't be both. But the *target* could be.
- **PtV users** — race them, but with Suicide/Det Mode you already have precog immunity yourself.
- **Verdict**: **MUTUAL CANCEL**. Both sides are precog-immune. Falls to other counters.

### 5. Anti-Trump shenanigans
- **Khepri (Tier 3, 15 SP)** — control the Trumper before they use their Trump
- **Power Manipulator yourself** — copy their FQ before they FQ you
- **Oberon yourself** — touch them, copy their FQ/Oberon, get the recursive FQ-stack
- **Verdict**: **MIRROR MATCH**. Whoever touches first wins.

### 6. **Trumped the Trump (HARD COUNTER)** ✅
- **Cost**: 3 SP (Perks and Drawbacks)
- **Effect**: "Unless you will it to be otherwise, **Trump powers do not work on you.** You can be Mastered into consenting to a Trump working on you, if you're not immune."
- **What it blocks**: FQ touch-steal, Oberon touch-copy, PM 1m-copy, Eidolon RNG powers, Powerswap, Fae Court, Trade!, Stronger Together, etc.
- **Verdict**: **HARD COUNTER**. For 3 SP, an entire Trump-stack (which costs 60–100+ SP to build) cannot copy, swap, or steal your powers. The only escape is Master-into-consent.
- **v1 ERROR**: This perk was MISSING from v1 entirely. The v1 conclusion that "no hard counter exists" was directly contradicted by source text.

### 7. Raw durability / Brute
- **Lung (Power Copy, 9 SP), (Brute/Breaker) Immortal (Tier 1, 30 SP), (Shaker) It Gets Worse (Tier 1, 40 SP)** — survive the touch
- **(Breaker) Phase (Tier 3, 2 SP)** — make the touch useless (you're gaseous; their powers phase through you)
- **(Breaker) Immune Response (Tier 3, 2 SP)** — immunity to physical effects
- **(Breaker) Gomu Gomu no Mi (Tier 3, 3 SP)** — Behemoth/Alexandria-tier blunt immunity
- **Verdict**: **PARTIAL counter**. Survives but doesn't win.

### 8. Tinker tech
- **Armsmaster, Bakuda, Dragon** — build anti-Trump tech
- **Manton-bypass weapons** — hit the cape without touching them
- **Verdict**: **WEAK counter**. Manton limits are explicit; non-Manton tech doesn't bypass touch requirements.

### 9. Conceptual / Reality-warper
- **(Trump) Pokemon (Tier 2, 30 SP)** — transform into something the touch can't reach
- **(Thinker) Path to Victory (Combo, 15 SP)** — path around the Trumper's touch (but PtV can't see Eidolon/Entities — see below)
- **(Shaker) It Gets Worse (Tier 1, 40 SP)** — undefined protection that may adapt to Trumps
- **(Trump) Fairy Queen (Tier 2, 25 SP)** — if the enemy has FQ, you can FQ them first
- **Verdict**: **STRONG counter**. If your power is "I win" then Trumps are just one of many things you win against.

### 10. Simurgh postcognition
- **Anti-precog immunity alone does NOT counter Simurgh.** She has pretercognition (precog + postcog combined), telekinesis, psychic scream (Master), and tinker-copying. Anti-precog blocks one sense; the others remain.
- **Verdict**: Simurgh is a meta-threat, not a Trump-specific counter.

## The hard-counter build (corrected from v1)

**Trumped the Trump** (3 SP) is THE hard counter. Here's a build that beats any Trump-stack:

```
Difficulty:  Easy (-30 SP) — accept the social lockout from Taylor/Amy/Lisa/Victoria
Shard:       Warrior Entity
Drawbacks:   Tinfoil Hat (+15), Fallen Worship (+15), Monologue (+13), Shounen (+13) = +56 SP, +46 CP

Starting budget: ~26 SP, 46 CP

Power Copy (1 pick):
  - Flechette/Foil                              10 SP   [Sting backup]

Tier 3 (5 picks, all cheap):
  - (Breaker) Gomu Gomu no Mi                  3 SP   [Behemoth/Alexandria blunt immune]
  - (Breaker) Phase                             2 SP   [bullet/laser immune; touch useless]
  - (Shaker) Target Acquired                    2 SP   [Stranger-detector within 300m]
  - (Blaster) Epilepsy                          1 SP   [10-second Thinker shutdown]
  - (Master) Geas                               1 SP   [enforceable contracts]

Perks (the counter):
  - Trumped the Trump                           3 SP   [HARD Trump counter]

TOTAL: ~22 SP, 46 CP — fits easily.
```

**Result:** A build with:
- **Trumped the Trump**: All Trump powers don't work on you. FQ can't touch-steal. Oberon can't touch-copy. PM can't 1m-copy. Eidolon gives you random powers but can't take yours.
- **Gomu Gomu + Phase**: Survive blunt and ranged. FQ's touch is useless (you're gaseous).
- **Target Acquired**: See through any Stranger hiding.
- **Epilepsy**: 10-second Thinker shutdown if a Precog spots you anyway.
- **Flechette/Foil backup**: If they attack, return Sting fire.

**Total cost: 22 SP.** Beats a Trump-stack that costs 60-100+ SP. The hard counter was sitting at 3 SP the whole time.

## Conclusion (corrected from v1)

**v1's conclusion was wrong.** There IS a hard counter to a fully-built Trump-stack: **Trumped the Trump (3 SP)** hard-counters all Trump powers on yourself. Combined with cheap survivability (Gomu Gomu + Phase + Target Acquired + Epilepsy), you can field a Trump-proof build for under 25 SP.

The CYOA explicitly allows Trump recursion (FQ-of-FQ, Oberon stacking with FQ). The strongest *Trump-on-Trump* counter is *another Trump-stack* who touches first. But the meta-game is: if your build can't be Trump'd at all (Trumped the Trump perk), you don't need to win the touch-race.

Updated meta:
- If you're a Trump-stack, your first move is to FQ Eidolon.
- If you're facing a Trump-stack **without Trumped the Trump**, your first move is to engage from range with a Master effect.
- If you're facing a Trump-stack **with Trumped the Trump (3 SP)**, your first move is to Master-into-consent (only escape the perk allows).
- If you don't want to play Trump-rock-paper-scissors, take Determination Mode (+100 SP) and use the wish on victory.
