## §1.0 — The three builds, summarized

### Build A: "The Cheap-Stack Survivor" (Vital Shard Baseline)

**The spider-man build.** Cheap tier-3 picks, broad coverage, big CP headroom for skills/identity.

```
Difficulty:    Determination Mode (+100 SP)
Drawbacks:     Tinfoil Hat (+15), Fallen Worship (+15),
               Case 53 (+13), Ditz (+7) = +50 SP, +30 CP
Shard:         Warrior Entity (default; avoid Thinker debt)
Shard Rank:    Vital (free; Tier 2 + 3)

Starting budget: 150 SP, 30 CP

TIER 3 POWERS (5 picks):
  (Master) Khepri              15 SP   [10m Master; portal-extension; Alexandria/Imp immune]
  (Breaker) Gomu Gomu no Mi     3 SP   [Alexandria-tier BLUNT immunity]
  (Breaker) Phase                2 SP   [BULLET & LASER immune while gaseous]
  (Shaker) Target Acquired       2 SP   [300m ANTI-STRANGER radar; reveals through walls]
  (Blaster) Epilepsy             1 SP   [10s THINKER shutdown per hit]
                                       = 23 SP

TIER 2 POWERS (3 picks):
  (Trump) Fairy Queen           25 SP   [touch-kill + store 3 capes as ghosts]
  (Trump) Eidolon               20 SP   [3 random + YOU are a precog blindspot]
  (Trump) Power Manipulator     20 SP   [1m copy; daily charges scale to 100%; 3 slots;
                                    copies ALL Entity powers separately]
                                       = 65 SP

COMBO POWERS (5 picks):
  Path to Victory               15 SP   [plans steps; HAS blindspots]
  The Legion of the Fae         15 SP   [50% copies of beaten capes, unlimited]
  The End-Becomer               10 SP   [control an existing Endbringer]
  Meta Knowledge                 5 SP   [detect simulation]
  Cabbage Corp                   5 SP   [sabotage with plausible deniability]
                                       = 50 SP

POWER COPY (1 pick):
  Coil                           7 SP   [dual-timeline backup]
                                       = 7 SP

PERKS:
  Trumped the Trump              3 SP   [HARD counter to all Trumps]
  All Seeing Precognition       20 SP + 10 CP   [removes PtV blindspots; only blindspot is Cloak V2]
  Cloak Mark II                  2 SP + 3 CP   [hides from human precogs INCLUDING All-Seeing]
  Cloak Mark IV                  2 SP + 1 CP   [hides from info-Thinkers (Tattletale works; Cherish/Khepri don't)]
  Noctis Cape                    3 SP   [no sleep + eidetic memory; PM charges faster]
                                       = 30 SP + 14 CP

SHARDLESS ONE-POINTERS (12 at 1 SP each = 12 SP):
  Perfect Aim, 3x reaction time, cape detection (Trump-class), underdog aura (Stranger),
  hair-control weapon, cold reading, friction control, monologues-inducing (Master), etc.

TOTAL: 187 SP + 14 CP
HEADROOM: -37 SP (over) — reduce by dropping Shardless (12 SP) or Legion of the Fae (15 SP)
ADJUSTED: 175 SP — fits with 5 SP budget slack
```

**Strengths**: Alexandria-blunt + bullet/laser immune, anti-Stranger radar, anti-Thinker tools, 10m Master, Eidolon blindspot-cloak, FQ/PM Trump-pair, hard Trump-counter via Trumped the Trump, PtV with blindspots removed, full Shardless coverage layer, 14 CP for skills.

**Weaknesses**: No Oberon/Titania (5-active touch-copy unavailable), no Sting Tier 1 (limited to Flechette's narrower Sting on nonliving only), no Immortal (death conditions possible if both Coronas destroyed), 187 SP is tight.

## §3.0 — The Game: "Threat Scenarios"

Here's how we play. **You choose a build**, then I narrate a threat scenario based on the Worm setting, then we resolve it. I'll be honest about what your build does well and what it doesn't.

The threats are drawn from `matchups.md`. The Cheap-Stack Survivor is roughly Build A (my recommended Vital baseline above with cheaper picks). The Vital Baseline is Build B. The Queen Maximilist is Build C.

### Pick your build

Tell me which one you want to run as your character for the scenarios:
- **A: Cheap-Stack Survivor** (175 SP, broad coverage, lots of skill headroom)
- **B: Vital Baseline** (177 SP, full Trump-stack core)
- **C: Queen Maximilist** (188 SP, Trump-stack with Oberon)

Then pick scenarios from this list, or I can run you through them in order:

### The scenarios

1. **"First Day in Brockton Bay"** — You trigger as a cape in 2011. Three goons from the ABB confront you in an alley. How do you handle this?

2. **"Lung Confrontation"** — Lung is mid-fight with the local Protectorate. He's been ramped for 4 minutes. You're nearby. What do you do?

3. **"Simurgh Attack"** — A Simurgh attack is incoming. You have 30 minutes. You're in downtown Brockton Bay. What's your move?

4. **"Trump-Stack vs Trump-Stack"** — You encounter another Trump user (assume they have FQ + Eidolon + PM + 3 Tier 3). What happens?

5. **"Scion Sighting"** — Scion appears in the distance. He's not moving toward you. What do you do?

6. **"Gold Morning"** — It's happening. Endbringers attacking simultaneously. How do you survive the first hour?

7. **"The Wish"** — You've defeated a major threat. Determination Mode wish activates. What do you wish for, and does your build's synergy matter?

### My role

I narrate the scenario, you describe what you do (or pick from your build's known capabilities). I'll honestly adjudicate:
- What your build's perks/powers do
- What your build's weaknesses expose
- How canon Worm threats actually behave
- Whether the wish interpretation matches your build

I'll be honest. If your build can't handle a threat, I'll say so. If your build has a clever interaction I missed, I'll acknowledge it.
