## Build role coverage analysis

Given a build's picks, here's how to evaluate coverage:

- A balanced build should hit at least 6 of: offense, defense, mobility, heal, precog, master, trump, stranger, thinker, construct
- Pure DPS builds can skip heal/stranger but need at least 2 of: defense, mobility, precog
- Social builds prioritize master + stranger + thinker + precog
- Master/Trump builds need at least 1 defense option to survive the inevitable counterattack

## 'I Win' button picks (cheap ways to trivialize the build)

- **Determination Mode** — wish on victory. The wish literally is the build.
- **(Trump) Oberon/Titania** (35 SP) — permanent copy of any cape you touch. Steal Eidolon's, Glaistig's, Contessa's.
- **(Trump) Fairy Queen** (25 SP) — kill a cape, take their power, store 3 at a time. Steal Eidolon + Glaistig + Contessa.
- **(Thinker) Path to Victory** (15 SP Combo) — Contessa-lite. **HAS BLINDSPOTS** (Eidolon, Trigger Events, Entities, Endbringers, etc.). Pair with All Seeing Precognition perk (20 SP + 10 CP) to remove blindspots.
- **(Brute/Breaker) Immortal** (30 SP) — immortal/invulnerable/inviolable. **Has a kill condition**: if both Corona Pollentia and Corona Gemma are near-simultaneously destroyed. Flechette can hurt you (but you regen instantly). Power runs out of juice eventually.
- **(Shaker) It Gets Worse** (40 SP) — undefined protection that auto-adapts.
