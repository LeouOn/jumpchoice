**For your build**: Take Golden Avatar. You get +30 SP/CP, Scion becomes permanently killable, AND he's weaker than canon Scion.

Golden Avatar is the only drawback in the CYOA that does three things at once:
1. **+30 SP/CP budget** (matches Avatar's tier)
2. **Locks Scion to Gold Morning level** (weaker than canon Scion)
3. **Avatar death = permanent death** (canonical Scion weakness removed)

Combined with Negentropy (Scion Trade), you have BOTH the diplomatic path AND the violent path. Combined with Eidolon/PM/FQ (Trump-stack), you can solo Scion even at Gold Morning level.

**Golden Avatar + Eidolon = unstoppable violent resolution**:
- Eidolon RNGs into strong powers
- PM copies ALL Entity powers separately (with Negentropy charging them to 100%)
- Gomu Gomu no Mi for Alexandria blunt immunity
- Phase for bullet/laser immunity
- Mental Barrier for Master/Stranger immunity
- Eidolon makes you a precog blindspot

**Golden Avatar + Negentropy = Scion Trade**:
- Walk up with Negentropy Shard
- Offer the trade: "Take my Shard, leave Sting and all others"
- Scion accepts per CYOA text
- Cycle ends without violence

You have **both options** in one build.

## §4.0 — Two playstyles in one build

### §4.1 — The Diplomat (Scion Trade)

**Setup**: You've played for 2+ years. You have Negentropy + Eidolon + FQ + PM all online. Eidolon RNG'd into useful powers. PM copied 3 Entity-powers at 100% strength (with Negentropy charging them instantly). FQ has 3 stolen capes in storage. You walk up to Scion.

**Step 1**: Eidolon makes you a precog blindspot. He can't see you coming.

**Step 2**: Mental Barrier protects from any Master/Stranger effect (Scion doesn't really have these but you have other threats).

**Step 3**: Walk up. He doesn't know what you want. PtV plans the conversation.

**Step 4**: "Scion. I have what you want. Look at my Shard."

His PtV-equivalent (golden light) scans you. He sees Negentropy in your Shard.

**Step 5**: He hesitates. The cycle has been running for eons. He never saw entropy defiance in a Shard.

**Step 6**: "Take my Shard. Leave Sting and all others. End the cycle."

**Step 7**: He accepts per CYOA text. He extracts your Negentropy Shard. He leaves Earth with the Shard. All other Shards (Sting, Eidolon, etc.) stay with humanity.

**Result**: Cycle ends. No Gold Morning. No Entities. No Endbringers. Humanity survives. You lose Negentropy but keep Eidolon/PM/FQ. **The negotiation succeeds.**

### §4.2 — The PAUNCH (Gomu Gomu no PAUNCH, violent resolution)

If diplomacy fails or you want to fight: **Golden Avatar + Eidolon + Negentropy = solo Scion**.

**Setup**: Same as above, but instead of walking up, you go PAUNCH.

**Step 1**: Eidolon makes you invisible to Scion's precog. He can't see you coming.

**Step 2**: Mental Barrier protects from any emotional/psychic attack.

**Step 3**: Walk up. PtV plans the attack sequence. (PtV blindspots include Entities, so PtV can't directly path Scion. But Eidolon makes you a blindspot to Scion's PtV too. So you can both see-and-not-be-seen.)

**Step 4**: PAUNCH. Gomu Gomu's "really elastic" body lets you stretch your fist. Alexandria-tier blunt force (Gomu Gomu) + Sting (Flechette or Eidolon-RNG'd copy) + Khepri (control Scion's body via 10m Master — wait, does Khepri work on Entities? Probably not, but it doesn't hurt).

**Step 5**: Eidolon has 3 random powers. If any of them are "kill entity" tier (canon Eidolon had stuff like Endbringer creation), use them. If not, Eidolon RNGs into a useful combat power.

**Step 6**: PM copied 3 Entity-powers at 100% strength. Use them.

**Step 7**: Scion is locked at Gold Morning level per Golden Avatar. He has emotions, can't disable them. He fights at full emotional weight, not cold PtV calculation.

**Step 8**: You damage his avatar. He tries to regenerate. **Per Golden Avatar, destroying the avatar kills him permanently.** No regeneration, no body-jumping.

**Result**: Scion dies. Permanently. The cycle ends. Humanity survives.
