# Worm CYOA V6.1 — v5 Build Redteam

> **Date:** 2026-06-20. An adversarial review of every major perk/power in the Vital Baseline v5 build. **Finds hidden weaknesses, exploit paths, and counter-builds** that the prior positive-framing analyses under-emphasized. Read this before committing to the v5 build.

## §1.0 — Build-level redteam (v5 as a whole)

### §1.1 — Strengths (recap)

- FQ + Eidolon + PM Trump-stack core (Tier 2, all 65 SP)
- Khepri (Tier 3, 10m Master, extends through portals)
- Gomu Gomu no Mi (Tier 3, Alexandria blunt immunity + electric immune + heat resistant)
- Phase (Tier 3, bullet/laser immune while gaseous)
- Trumped the Trump (3 SP, anti-Trump-stack)
- Mental Barrier (1 SP + 5 CP, full Master/Stranger immunity)
- Cloak Mark IV (anti-info-Thinker)
- Noctis Cape (no sleep + eidetic memory)
- Target Acquired (300m anti-Stranger radar)
- Negentropy (energy-free + Scion Trade win condition)
- Golden Avatar (Scion locked + permanently killable)
- Plot Armor (no accident deaths)
- May you live in Interesting Times (constant fights)
- Second Wave Parahumans (new capes globally)

### §1.2 — Build-level weaknesses

**Weakness 1: No Tier 1 durability.** Immortal (30 SP, Tier 1) would have given you true immortality. Instead you rely on Gomu Gomu (blunt) + Phase (ranged) + Mental Barrier (control). A determined Brute with bio-modification or cutting attacks bypasses Gomu Gomu. A precise Tinker ranged attack bypasses Phase. A Mind-control Tinker trick could theoretically bypass Mental Barrier (though it's labeled "full immunity").

**Weakness 2: Eidolon RNG can fail.** "Somewhat-random" is vague. If Eidolon rolls 3 weak powers (Shaker 1 / Mover 2 / Striker 3), your build's apex is gone. Negentropy fixes the energy issue but not the RNG.

**Weakness 3: PM is a long-game build.** "Every day you get a charge" — takes 1-4 weeks to fully upgrade a power. In a crisis on Day 30, your PM has 2-4 charges. **PM is not a frontline combatant until Month 3+.** Your early game is Khepri + FQ + Gomu Gomu + Phase only.

**Weakness 4: Khepri 10m range is melee.** "Within 10 meters of yourself." Outside 10m, no control. **You have to get close to a cape to control them.** That's dangerous against long-range Blasters, Movers, Strangers.

**Weakness 5: Eidolon is "a precognitive blindspot to everyone but the entity."** This means Contessa CAN see your Eidolon-stolen powers if she paths them through Scion. **Eidolon blindspot is not full precog immunity.**

**Weakness 6: PtV HAS blindspots.** Eidolon, Trigger Events, Entities, Parts of Entities, Endbringers, etc. Without ASP, your PtV literally cannot path the meta-threats (Scion, Endbringers). You have Eidolon-blindspot + Cloak II/III/IV but **PtV cannot plan against Eidolon himself or the Entities.**

**Weakness 7: FQ ghosts are "caricature" form.** "all active powers take the form of a ghostly projection that somewhat resembles their living form, if in the form of a caricature." This suggests the ghosts are visibly ghost-like and possibly distinguishable from the original cape. **This affects stealth/roleplay scenarios.**

**Weakness 8: No ranged offense built-in.** Your build has FQ (touch), Khepri (10m), Eidolon (RNG), PM (1m copy). **You don't have a reliable long-range attack.** Flechette is in Power Copy but you didn't take it (you took Coil). Eidolon might RNG a Blaster. If not, you're a melee brawler.

**Weakness 9: Mental Barrier is "full immunity" but Eidolon stole "all his powers" might include Master powers.** Eidolon can RNG a Master power (Cherish, Regent, etc.). Does Mental Barrier block YOUR OWN Master power usage? The text says "immune to any and all Master and Stranger effects" — this should include your own.

**Weakness 10: Golden Avatar has "all his powers, to some degree or another."** Scion at Gold Morning power level is still incredibly strong. He had ~50 capes fighting him and he was killing them. Your build is one cape. **The "killable" attribute requires you to actually kill him, which is still hard.**

## §2.0 — Per-perk redteam (every major element)

### §2.1 — Trumped the Trump (3 SP)

**Text**: "Unless you will it to be otherwise, Trump powers do not work on you. **You can be Mastered into consenting to a Trump working on you, if you're not immune.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **The Master-consent clause is a built-in backdoor.** If you don't have Master immunity, an enemy can Master you into turning Trumped the Trump off. With Mental Barrier, this is moot. Without Mental Barrier, this is a critical vulnerability.
2. **Trumped the Trump only blocks Trump-class powers.** Non-Trump Master effects (Cherish's emotion-control) still work without Mental Barrier.
3. **It doesn't stack with Eidolon-blindspot.** Eidolon makes you a precog blindspot, which is a different mechanic. Eidolon-blindspot doesn't block Trumps.

**Counter-counter**: Mental Barrier (1 SP + 5 CP) closes the Master-consent backdoor. With both, you have full Trump+Master immunity.

### §2.2 — Mental Barrier (1 SP + 5 CP)

**Text**: "You are immune to any and all Master and Stranger effects. You are also immune to other emotional effects caused directly by powers, such as those of Victoria Dallon's emotional aura."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Doesn't cover Tinker effects.** Bonesaw's plagues, Armsmaster's halberd, Dragon's tech — all still work.
2. **Doesn't cover Thinker info-derivation.** Tattletale works. Number Man works.
3. **Doesn't cover Precog.** Contessa paths you. Dinah sees percentages. Coil splits timelines.
4. **Doesn't cover natural human social pressure.** You can still be persuaded, intimidated, or manipulated by mundane means (assuming Shounen drawback isn't active).
5. **The "emotional effects caused directly by powers" is bounded.** If an effect is indirect (Master triggers a non-Master's actions), it's not covered. For example: Regent controls a non-Master, who shoots you — the bullet is mechanical, not Master.

**Counter-counter**: Cloak Mark IV (info-Thinkers), ASP (precog), Eidolon-blindspot (Contessa-specific).

### §2.3 — Negentropy (8 SP + 3 CP)

**Text**: "It somehow went unnoticed by the entities that your Shard had the ability they sought for eons: it defies entropy. Somehow your power allows you to defy entropy, and uses this itself to keep it charged. **You will never run out of energy.** **This is more or less useless unless you're either immortal or think you're capable of convincing the entity to leave behind all his other Shards, including Sting**, and therefore not kill all humans in exchange for your Shard."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **The CYOA text says it's "more or less useless" without Immortal or the Scion Trade.** This is an explicit statement from the CYOA author that Negentropy's value is conditional. Without one of these conditions, Negentropy is reduced to "energy-free shard powers" which is a buff but not a win condition.
2. **Doesn't give you NEW powers.** It just makes your existing powers never run out. If your Eidolon rolls bad, Negentropy doesn't fix that.
3. **The Scion Trade is not guaranteed.** The CYOA says "think you're capable of convincing the entity." This is up to the player roleplay. **Scion may refuse. Scion may attack first.** The Trade is a possibility, not a guarantee.
4. **You lose the Shard in the Trade.** Negentropy is your Shard. If you trade it, you lose Negentropy AND the Shard that powers your other abilities. **The Trade costs you everything.**

**Counter-counter**: Pair with Immortal (Tier 1, 30 SP) so you don't lose Negentropy's value when you trade it (you're already immortal). Or don't trade — just use Negentropy as an energy buff.

### §2.4 — Golden Avatar (-23 SP -7 CP)

**Text**: "The Warrior Entity is trapped in his avatar. He is still capable of using all his powers, to some degree or another, but he cannot disable the facsimile of human emotions he feels, and **destroying the Avatar will kill him permanently.** He is stuck fighting on the level he was at during Gold Morning."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"All his powers, to some degree or another"** is vague. Does this include his full golden light beams? Full PtV-equivalent? Full regeneration? The text is ambiguous. If "to some degree or another" means "reduced," Gold Morning Scion might be weaker than I assumed. If it means "to any degree he chooses," he's full-power minus emotion-disable.
2. **Gold Morning Scion was killing dozens of capes at once.** Your build is one cape. The PAUNCH path is harder than my analysis suggested. You need Eidolon RNG + PM copies + Gomu Gomu + Flechette/Sting to even have a chance.
3. **"Stuck fighting on the level he was at during Gold Morning"** means Scion is at a specific power level. **Is this canon Gold Morning Scion (multi-endbringer fight, peak power) or pre-GM Scion (weaker)?** Ambiguous. If it's peak GM Scion, this is the hardest version of Scion. If it's pre-GM Scion (no emotional defeat yet), this is still extremely strong.
4. **"Destroying the Avatar will kill him permanently"** requires actually destroying the avatar. Scion has golden light beams, PtV-equivalent, and presumably full power-with-emotions. **The fight is real.**

**Counter-counter**: Eidolon RNG + PM copies + Negentropy-charged powers + help from capes (this is canon Gold Morning — you don't solo this without allies).

### §2.5 — Eidolon (Tier 2, 20 SP)

**Text**: "You have Eidolon's powers without the worthy opponent issue. You can have three somewhat-random powers that you can change at any time you want. These powers can be very powerful. You can switch out quality for quantity and have five weaker powers instead of three stronger ones. **All powers take a couple of minutes to build up strength**, though they're still usable before reaching full strength. **You can still run out of energy with this power.** You're a precognitive blindspot to everyone but the entity."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Somewhat-random"** — what pool? PRT rating 1-10? Any tier? Any class? The CYOA doesn't specify. Worst case: 3 PRT-3 powers (e.g., Tattletale-tier Thinker + small Brute + small Blaster). Best case: 3 Endbringer-class powers. **RNG is brutal.**
2. **"All powers take a couple of minutes to build up strength"** — opening minutes of any fight, your Eidolon powers are weaker than full strength. Surprise attacks exploit this.
3. **"You can still run out of energy with this power"** — Negentropy fixes this. Without Negentropy, Eidolon fades.
4. **"You're a precognitive blindspot to everyone but the entity"** — Scion can see you. Contessa can see your Eidolon (and path around Eidolon via Scion).
5. **3 random powers OR 5 weaker powers** — the quality-quantity tradeoff is textually present but I haven't seen anyone analyze the 5-weaker tradeoff. 5 weaker powers of "very powerful" tier might still be strong, but "weaker" is undefined.

**Counter-counter**: Negentropy fixes energy. Eidolon-blindspot blocks PtV except Scion. Play the Eidolon RNG offensively — use the first 3-5 minutes for non-Eidolon combat (Khepri + Gomu Gomu), then Eidolon powers ramp up.

### §2.6 — Power Manipulator (Tier 2, 20 SP)

**Text**: "You have the ability to copy extremely weakened versions of powers off Parahumans and a slot to use those powers with. **To copy a Parahuman's power you must get within a meter of them.** **If you copy the power of an Endbringer or Entity, you get every single power they have separately, instead of just one power.** You can switch what power is in your slots at will. **Every day you get a charge you can use to increase the strength of a power**, though you can save these charges as well. Once a power reaches the same strength as the original power, you can no longer dump charges into it. **On average it takes a week to fully charge a weak power, two weeks to fully upgrade a mid-level power, three weeks to fully upgrade a high-level power, and four weeks to fully upgrade an extremely powerful power.** You can also use those charges to increase how many slots you have, **to a maximum of three.** It takes 30 charges to fully increase the number of slots you have..."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **1m copy range.** You have to get within arm's reach. That's melee range. If you copy an Entity, you're dead first.
2. **"Extremely weakened versions"** — copied powers are weak initially. Daily charges scale them up but that takes weeks.
3. **Entity copy requires you to survive being within 1m of an Entity.** Even with Mental Barrier + Eidolon-blindspot + Gomu Gomu, being 1m from Scion is suicide.
4. **Long-term scaling is the value.** Early-game PM is one weak slot. By Month 3 you have 3 slots at 100% strength. By Month 6 you've copied every cape in Brockton Bay at full strength.
5. **PM copied powers are your copies, not the originals.** The capes you copied still have their powers. This isn't a theft — it's a copy.

**Counter-counter**: Negentropy accelerates charges. Eidolon can RNG a Thinker power to identify targets faster. PM + Eidolon = mid-to-late-game godmode.

### §2.7 — Fairy Queen (Tier 2, 25 SP)

**Text**: "You have Glaistig Uaine's powerset. With a touch you can kill a Parahuman and steal their power. **You can steal their power from their corpse.** You can only have three active stolen powers at a time, and all active powers take the form of a ghostly projection that somewhat resembles their living form, **if in the form of a caricature.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Touch-kill required.** FQ is melee. Long-range Blasters, Movers, Strangers are immune (you can't touch them).
2. **3-active cap.** You can only have 3 ghosts at a time. To steal more, you have to release one.
3. **"Caricature" form.** The ghosts are visibly ghost-like, probably distinguishable from the original cape. **This affects social/roleplay scenarios** (you can't pretend the ghost IS the original cape).
4. **Killing is permanent.** FQ ghosts are "permanently" yours (per Legion of the Fae synergy), but the capes you killed are dead. This has moral weight.
5. **Stolen powers are at unknown strength.** Does FQ give you 100% strength of the stolen power, or a percentage? The text is silent. **Likely 100%** but unverified.

**Counter-counter**: Eidolon can RNG a Master power to control capes from range, so you don't have to FQ touch-kill everything. Phase gets you into melee range. Gomu Gomu lets you survive the touch.

### §2.8 — Oberon/Titania (Tier 1, 35 SP) — NOT in v5 build but referenced

**Text**: "You can gain a permanent copy of the power of any Parahuman you touch. This works on the substance in Cauldron Vials and the Garden of Eden too, though adapted for human use. **You can only have five of these powers active at a time**, but rather than the Fairy Queen's ghosts, you have these powers for yourself."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **5-active cap.** You can copy unlimited capes but only 5 are active.
2. **Requires touch.** Same melee problem as FQ.
3. **"Adapted for human use"** — Cauldron vials give diluted powers.
4. **Queen shard required.** Not available with Vital shard.

### §2.9 — Path to Victory (Combo, 15 SP)

**Text**: "You can come up with a step-by-step plan to fulfill any goal, and can perform each step perfectly. **Blindspots include Eidolon, Trigger Events, Entities, Parts of Entities, Endbringers, etc.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Etc."** — the blindspot list isn't exhaustive. There may be more blindspots not listed.
2. **The listed blindspots are exactly the meta-threats.** Without ASP, PtV is useless against Scion, Endbringers, Eidolon, Trigger Events. You have Eidolon-blindspot yourself but PtV still can't path against Eidolon-class threats.
3. **PtV returns steps but the steps might be impossible.** "Path to victory" doesn't mean "easy path" or "no-cost path." The steps could be sacrificial.
4. **"You can perform each step perfectly"** has a literal reading: you can execute the steps. But the steps might require resources you don't have (allies, time, location).

**Counter-counter**: ASP (20 SP + 10 CP) removes blindspots. Eidolon-blindspot partially compensates. Plan around blindspots using Eidolon RNG.

### §2.10 — Khepri (Tier 3, 15 SP)

**Text**: "You have absolute control of the bodies and powers of every carbon based lifeform within 10 meters of yourself, as well as an unlimited ability to multitask. This power extends through portals. **You will retain your sanity.** You can turn this power off at will, or let people within your range keep control of themselves while you control others. **Certain people are immune to this power, such as Alexandria and Imp.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **10m range.** Outside 10m, no control. **Long-range Blasters/Movers can kite you.**
2. **"Certain people are immune"** — Alexandria (Manton-limit), Imp (Stranger), and probably others. **The text lists two but says "such as" implying more.**
3. **"Extends through portals"** — requires a portal to be nearby. Without a portal-generator in the area, range is 10m.
4. **"Carbon based lifeform"** — Shatterbird (silicon-based powers) might be excluded.
5. **"You will retain your sanity"** — canon Khepri lost sanity. This is a buff. But "retain sanity" might mean "you don't go insane," not "you don't suffer psychological effects." Ambiguous.
6. **Master-effects on you?** Khepri in canon was vulnerable to PtV-blindspotted capes. Are capes in your 10m range immune to Khepri because they're ptv-blindspotted? Unclear.

**Counter-counter**: Mental Barrier protects you from Master effects. Eidolon-blindspot hides you from Precog. Use Khepri strategically — get close, control, get out.

### §2.11 — Gomu Gomu no Mi (Tier 3, 3 SP)

**Text**: "You are a rubber person. Or, really, just really elastic and not conductive. **Blunt forces do no damage to you** and you're resistant to heat. **You could get crushed by Behemoth or punched by Alexandria and not get damaged.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Resistant to heat" not "immune to heat."** Heat still hurts, just less. Burnscar (Tier 3, 5 SP) might be a problem.
2. **Doesn't cover cutting/piercing.** Flechette's Sting projectiles might still hurt you (Sting ignores "select laws of physics" — is rubber "select"? Ambiguous).
3. **Doesn't cover energy/conceptual.** Energy blasts, conceptual attacks, etc. — undefined.
4. **"Really elastic"** — if you stretch limbs for offense, the extended limb is vulnerable to being cut/attacked.
5. **Rubber person** in One Piece has specific weaknesses: **cutting attacks work** (e.g., Mihawk's swords), **lightning can still hurt** (though not conductive, heat damage), **seastone negates rubber powers**. The CYOA doesn't mention seastone, but cutting is implied.

**Counter-counter**: Phase for cutting/energy attacks. Mental Barrier for Master effects. Eidolon can RNG a Brute/Tinker power for layered defense.

### §2.12 — Phase (Tier 3, 2 SP)

**Text**: "You can shift into a semi-gaseous state. While in this state, can can pass through walls as long as some part of you other than your feet go first. You can technically phase through the floor, but as long as your feet touch the floor first, you won't phase through it. **You remain vulnerable to fire and electricity, and are especially vulnerable to strong winds. Strong winds won't kill you, but you can't exactly resist them. You are immune to bullets and lasers while in this state; they'll past straight through you.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Vulnerable to fire, electricity, and strong winds.** Three major elementals bypass Phase.
2. **Feet-first phasing rule.** If you're lifted off the ground (Mover, telekinesis), the rule might break.
3. **"Semi-gaseous"** — semi-gaseous, not fully gaseous. Bullets pass through but some attacks might still hurt.
4. **"While in this state"** — toggleable. If you're not in this state, normal vulnerabilities.
5. **"Especially vulnerable to strong winds"** — wind can push you around, into walls, off buildings. Tactical vulnerability.

**Counter-counter**: Gomu Gomu (heat resistant, lightning not conductive) provides complementary defense. Eidolon can RNG a Mover power to counter wind.

### §2.13 — Biobringer (Combo, 10 SP)

**Text**: "You have absolute control and understanding of all carbon based lifeforms. **You also can control biology at three times the rate you could before.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Carbon based"** — silicon-based (Shatterbird), tech-based, energy-based, conceptual-based are all excluded.
2. **Three-times-rate** — vague. Is this three times as many targets? Three times the speed of control? Unclear.
3. **Requires biological target.** Non-living things (machines, energy constructs, conceptual entities) aren't affected.
4. **Range undefined.** Does it work at 10m like Khepri? Or globally? The text is silent. Probably has a range limit but undefined.

**Counter-counter**: Pair with Khepri (10m Master) for layered bio-control. Eidolon can RNG bio-control powers. PM can copy Entities' powers.

### §2.14 — End-Becomer (Combo, 10 SP)

**Text**: "You can take control over any single one of the existing Endbringers, while keeping all your other powers as well as getting theirs."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Any single one"** — you control ONE Endbringer. There are multiple (Leviathan, Simurgh, Tohu, Bohu, Khonsu — plus post-GM Behemoth's death doesn't recreate).
2. **"Existing"** — if an Endbringer is destroyed, the perk doesn't make a new one.
3. **"Getting theirs"** — the controlled Endbringer's powers are yours too. **This is huge but also dangerous** — you now have Endbringer-class power but the Endbringer's psychological state (Simurgh's screaming) might persist.
4. **Control mechanism undefined.** Do you command the Endbringer or do you BECOME the Endbringer? The text says "take control" which suggests the former, but "getting theirs" suggests fusion.

**Counter-counter**: Eidolon can RNG a Master power for Endbringer control (End-Becomer is just a free version of that). Khepri in 10m controls Endbringers' bodies (Shards are carbon-based? Maybe).

### §2.15 — Legion of the Fae (Combo, 15 SP)

**Text**: "If you've beaten a Parahuman in battle, **you permanently have the 50% as powerful version of their power**, even when you have a 100% as powerful power active. You can have an unlimited number of those 50% as powerful powers active."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **"Beaten in battle"** — you have to actually fight them and win. A lucky Eidolon RNG that helps you beat them isn't a "fight."
2. **"50% as powerful version"** — half-strength. For top-tier capes (Eidolon, Alexandria), 50% is still strong. For PRT-3 capes, 50% is weak.
3. **"Unlimited count"** — but each is 50%. Quantity doesn't make up for quality in raw power terms.
4. **"Beaten in battle"** — does it require you to FQ-kill them? Or just defeat them? If just defeat, you can build this up over time. If FQ-kill, you double-dip (FQ + Legion).

**Counter-counter**: Negentropy keeps the 50% copies active forever. Eidolon RNG strong powers.

### §2.16 — Cloak Marks I-IV

**Cloak I** (1 SP + 2 CP): "Thinkers cannot find out that you are from another universe that has a multiversal-level unrestricted Precog." Blocks Thinkers discovering your origin. Doesn't block other Thinker powers.

**Cloak II** (2 SP + 3 CP): "You are a blind spot to human precogs, including those with All-Seeing Precognition." Blocks human precog. Doesn't block Entity/Simurgh precog.

**Cloak III** (3 SP + 2 CP): "You are a blind spot to Endbringer and Entity precognitives." Blocks Endbringer/Entity precog. Doesn't block human precog.

**Cloak IV** (2 SP + 1 CP): "You are a blind spot to Thinker powers that get someone's information using anything other than environmental data or what the Thinker already knows. **Tattletale's power would still work on you, but Cherish's or Khepri's wouldn't.**" Blocks info-Thinkers EXCEPT Tattletale.

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Cloak IV doesn't block Tattletale.** She uses environmental data. Even with Cloak IV, she knows.
2. **Cloak II doesn't block Simurgh precog.** Simurgh is non-human Entity. You need Cloak III for Simurgh specifically.
3. **No Cloak blocks Eidolon-blindspot.** Eidolon is a Trump, not a Precog. Eidolon-blindspot is a separate mechanic.
4. **Cloak Mark I only blocks origin discovery.** Doesn't block other Thinker effects.

**Counter-counter**: ASP (20 SP + 10 CP) makes your PtV see everything AND removes your own PtV blindspots. Eidolon-blindspot covers Trumps.

### §2.17 — Noctis Cape (3 SP)

**Text**: "You have no need to sleep, you'll constantly be alert and awake. **You also have a perfectly eidetic memory, capable of perfectly recalling anything that has happened to you since you received your powers.** Any memories prior to your trigger are remembered as well as you could recall them when you triggered."

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Eidetic memory = perfect recall.** Every trauma, every horror, every defeat — perfectly remembered. **Psychological weight.**
2. **"Always alert"** — no downtime. Burnout is real even without sleep.
3. **Doesn't grant better processing.** Eidetic memory ≠ eidetic recall speed. You remember everything but can't necessarily access it faster.
4. **Doesn't help with Eidolon RNG.** Eidolon rolls randomly, not from your memory.

### §2.18 — Target Acquired (Tier 3, 2 SP)

You can decide if others can see the glow, or if you only want specific people to see it."

2. **Toggleable** — if you turn it off, no glow.
3. **"They can even be seen through walls or if they're invisible"** — this is great but doesn't help if the threat is more than 300m away.

### §2.19 — Plot Armor (5 CP)

**Text**: "You're not unlucky. **You won't get killed by accident, only by something someone did intentionally.** Someone has to go out of their way to kill you if they want you dead. **This power doesn't protect you from Endbringers.**"

**Weaknesses**:
1. **Doesn't protect from Endbringers.** Endbringers are explicitly excluded.
2. **"Someone has to go out of their way"** — most villains in Worm DO go out of their way. This is essentially "no accidental deaths."
3. **Doesn't make you harder to kill.** Just removes the "random bad luck" tier of deaths.

## §3.0 — Synergy redteam (where the build's pieces DON'T combine well)

### §3.1 — Eidolon-blindspot vs. PtV

**Claimed synergy**: Eidolon makes you a precog blindspot to everyone but the entity. PtV plans steps.

**Reality**: Eidolon-blindspot is a passive. PtV is active. They don't combine — they're independent. **If Contessa paths your death, your Eidolon-blindspot means she can't see you directly. But she can path "redirect Sisyphus to a trap" without seeing you.** PtV is supposed to counter this but PtV HAS blindspots including Entities (which Eidolon blindspot doesn't help with).

### §3.2 — FQ + Eidolon RNG recursion

**Claimed synergy**: FQ stores Eidolon's power. You get Eidolon's 3 random + FQ's 3 stolen.

**Reality**: Eidolon's 3 random ARE Eidolon's powers. FQ stores "their power" — Eidolon's actual stored power-set. **You can't FQ Eidolon-stolen powers to stack them.** You're storing Eidolon himself, not his stored powers. Recursion is real but doesn't compound.

### §3.3 — PM + Negentropy charging

**Claimed synergy**: Negentropy makes charges infinite. PM charges scale to 100%.

**Reality**: Negentropy makes PM "never run out of energy." But PM's charges are a daily mechanic, not energy. **"Every day you get a charge" — the daily recharge isn't an energy limit.** Negentropy might not affect this. **Verify in-game.**

### §3.4 — Mental Barrier + Eidolon-stolen Master powers

**Claimed synergy**: Mental Barrier = full Master immunity. Eidolon can RNG a Master power for offense.

**Reality**: Mental Barrier text: "You are immune to any and all Master and Stranger effects." **Does this include your own Eidolon-stolen Master power?** If Eidolon RNGs Cherish, can YOU use Cherish's emotion-control on others? If Mental Barrier blocks all Master effects, including your own, then Eidolon-stolen Master powers don't work for you.

**This is a critical ambiguity I missed.** If Mental Barrier blocks your own Master powers, Eidolon's value drops. If Mental Barrier only blocks incoming Master effects, Eidolon's Master powers work.

### §3.5 — Golden Avatar + Scion Trade

**Claimed synergy**: Golden Avatar locks Scion. Negentropy allows Scion Trade.

**Reality**: Golden Avatar locks Scion at Gold Morning power level. **Scion has emotions.** He's vulnerable to manipulation. The Trade offer (Negentropy for Sting + all other Shards) requires Scion to agree. **An emotional Scion might refuse the Trade out of spite.** Or he might accept. Unpredictable.

### §3.6 — Khepri + Eidolon-stolen Bio-control

**Claimed synergy**: Khepri controls carbon-based life. Eidolon can RNG Panacea's biokinesis. Together: full bio-control.

**Reality**: Khepri controls carbon-based life FORMS (bodies). Biokinesis controls biology at the cellular level. They're different layers. **You don't get a synergy bonus** — they're independent bio-control methods.

## §7.0 — Verdict on v5

**v5 is strong but has hidden vulnerabilities.**

**Strengths (unchanged)**:
- FQ + Eidolon + PM Trump-stack core

- Eidolon-blindspot doesn't help vs. Contessa-paths-through-Scion

- Mental Barrier vs. Eidolon-Master ambiguity
- Khepri's "such as" immunity list
- Golden Avatar's "to some degree or another" ambiguity

**For a player who wants the v5 build**: take it. The strengths outweigh the weaknesses. The ambiguities (Mental Barrier vs. Eidolon-Master, Golden Avatar's "to some degree") are edge cases you can adjudicate at the table.

**For a player who wants a tighter build**: swap Eidolon for Flechette/Foil (ranged offense guaranteed), add Immortal (Tier 1, 30 SP) for true immortality, drop Khepri for Eidolon-stolen Master redundancy.
