# Worm CYOA V6.1 — Second Opinion (Cheap Perks, Canon Corrections, Revised Takes)

**Reviewer's preamble:** This doc is a course-correction. My `critical-review.md` over-swung on a few things — most egregiously, I dismissed the cheap perk/power tier too aggressively. The user correctly pushed back: **"Perfect Aim can be good you know... there are lots of cheap perks that are pretty strong."** They're right. This doc re-evaluates cheap picks properly, then folds in canon corrections from a fresh research pass.

The strategic insight the user is pointing at: **coverage beats peak.** A build with 20 cheap powers covering 20 situations is more robust than a build with 3 expensive powers covering 3 situations. In a setting as diverse as Worm (Master, Stranger, Trump, Brute, Endbringer, Entity, S9, PRT, social, biological, dimensional...), breadth wins.

## §2.0 — Major canon corrections from the research pass

A librarian agent verified Worm canon against my prior claims. Several major errors confirmed:

### §2.1 — Sting alone does NOT kill Entities ⚠️

**Prior claim** (`matchups.md`, `critical-review.md`): "Sting is the actual Entity-killer."

**Canon reality:** Sting was **necessary but not sufficient**. The actual kill chain on Scion was:
1. **Psychological warfare** — Oliver's power made Scion briefly see Eden, then the heroes crushed that hope. Scion gave up and stopped using PtV.
2. **Foil imbued bolts with Sting**; Ballistic fired them into Scion's avatar.
3. Sting **"wedged open" the portal** between Scion's avatar and his real body / main mass.
4. A **collaborative Tinker superweapon** (Defiant + many tinkers) fired through that opened gateway and destroyed the Warrior's real body.

**Translation for the CYOA:** Sting Tier 1 (25 SP) **breaches Entity defenses** but doesn't solo-kill them. You still need a Tinker superweapon or comparable damage source to fire through the breach. **Flechette alone is not the answer; Flechette + Tinker + psychological warfare is.**

### §2.2 — Khepri Combo is NOT in base V6.1 ⚠️

**Prior claim** (`combo-encyclopedia.md`, `archetypes.md`): "Khepri (Combo, 15 SP)."

**Canon/CYOA reality:** The librarian searched the base V6.1 source. **Khepri appears only once** — inside the Cloak Mark IV perk description. **There is no standalone Khepri Combo in base V6.1.** The 15-SP Khepri combo is from **mods** (Brigade's mod, Maximus's mod).

However — and this is important — **`(Master) Khepri` IS in the base V6.1 as a Tier 3 power** (15 SP, 10m range, portal-extensible). My `critical-review.md` corrected the row placement but the encyclopedia and archetypes docs still treat it as a Combo.

**Fix:** Update `combo-encyclopedia.md` to remove Khepri. Update `archetypes.md` builds that counted Khepri as a Combo slot.

### §2.3 — "Restricted Contessa" has no canon basis ⚠️

**Prior claim** (`critical-review.md` §1, etc.): The CYOA's Determination Mode "restricted Contessa" maps to canon.

**Canon reality:** Eidolon's death did **not** restrict Contessa. Her blindspots are hardcoded by Eden, not contingent on Eidolon being alive. The CYOA's "restricted power" line is a **game-balance invention**, not a canon event. Most defensible reading: "restricted" means *you* (with Cloak) are a blindspot to her, not that her power is intrinsically nerfed.

### §2.4 — Glaistig Uaine doesn't strictly need to kill ⚠️

**Prior claim** (`trump-stack.md`, `critical-review.md`): "FQ requires murder."

**Canon reality:** Glaistig Uaine can claim parahumans **who died in her vicinity** — not only those she kills personally. The CYOA's "you can steal their power from their corpse" clause actually implies this. So FQ is more flexible than I claimed: you can scavenge battlefields.

### §2.5 — Canon Lung = Brute/Blaster only (no Mover/Changer) ⚠️

**Canon reality:** Lung's PRT designation is **Brute 4-9, Blaster 2-6 (fire/heat only)**. No Mover rating, no Changer rating (Wildbow explicitly noted Changer isn't applied to Lung — his transformation is folded into Brute). His functional abilities include wings/flight at full ramp, but the classification is Brute/Blaster.

**CYOA Power Copy text:** "You start as a low level brute but grow stronger and slowly transform into a dragon the longer you fights. You have pyrokinesis and enhanced senses." — So the CYOA gives the **full escalating package**, not just a Brute slice. Good.

### §2.6 — Eidolon's "no worthy opponent" is a BUFF, not neutral ⚠️

**Canon reality:** Wildbow WoG states Eidolon's power loss was partly because he was "hooked up to three (later five) giant energy drains" — the Endbringers themselves were draining him. By removing the worthy-opponent issue, the CYOA **also removes the drain**. So CYOA Eidolon is stronger than canon Eidolon.

### §2.7 — The Simurgh is NOT fully countered by anti-precog ⚠️

**Canon reality:** The Simurgh has **pretercognition** = precognition + postcognition. Plus telekinesis, psychic scream (Master effect), and Tinker-copying. Anti-precog immunity blocks ONE of her senses. She can still:
- Read your past (postcognition)
- Hit you with telekinesis (pure physics)
- Scream at you (Master effect)
- Reverse-engineer Tinker tech from past data

**Fix:** My `blindspots.md` claimed "Suicide/Det Mode + PtV = Simurgh-proof." That's an overclaim. You'd also need anti-Master, anti-Stranger, and ideally postcog-blocking.

## §7.0 — Takeaway

The prior analysis (mine included) systematically **over-weighted expensive flash** and **under-weighted cheap coverage.** The user's instinct is correct: in a setting as diverse as Worm, a build with 15 cheap powers that cover 15 threats beats a build with 3 expensive powers that cover 3 threats perfectly and 12 threats not at all.

**The most under-rated picks in the CYOA, by a wide margin, are:**
1. **(Breaker) Gomu Gomu no Mi** (3 SP) — Alexandria-tier blunt immunity
2. **(Blaster) Epilepsy** (1 SP) — Thinker shutdown
3. **Trumped the Trump** (3 SP) — Trump immunity
4. **(Shardless) Perfect Aim** (1 SP) — with Sting, devastating
5. **(Breaker) Phase** (2 SP) — bullet/laser immunity

These five picks total **10 SP** and provide more practical survivability than (Brute/Breaker) Immortal at 30 SP. The budget math changes completely when you take them seriously.
