# Worm CYOA V6.1 — Matchup Analysis (v2 — Repaired)

> **Repaired 2026-06-20.** v1 had multiple errors: claimed Contessa/Doormaker/Legend/Crawler/Glaistig Uaine were pickable when they're NOT, claimed Khepri was a Combo when it's Tier 3, claimed Sting alone kills Scion when canon requires psychological warfare + Tinker superweapon + Sting, said "PtV has no blindspots" when it explicitly does. v2 fixes all.

How do various builds fare against the major Worm threats?

## Important framing note

**PtV has explicit blindspots**: "Blindspots include Eidolon, Trigger Events, Entities, Parts of Entities, Endbringers, etc." PtV cannot path around Entities or Endbringers. To remove these, pair PtV with the perk **All Seeing Precognition** (20 SP + 10 CP). To hide from All Seeing, use **Cloak Mark II** (2 SP + 3 CP).

**Endbringers are NOT mind-controllable by Khepri** in canon (Taylor's QA controls parahumans via Corona Pollentia; Endbringers are shard-constructs). The CYOA's `(Master) Khepri` (Tier 3, 15 SP) doesn't fix this — it just grants the body-control mechanic. The Endbringers canonically helped during Gold Morning of their own accord (the Simurgh voluntarily worked with Tattletale). The CYOA's `The End-Becomer` (Combo, 10 SP) bypasses this by granting direct Endbringer control, but that's a CYOA invention, not a canon Khepri mechanic.

**Sting alone does NOT kill Scion.** The canon kill required (a) psychological warfare breaking Scion's will, (b) Sting-infused bolts to wedge open the avatar-to-Entity portal, (c) a collaborative Tinker superweapon fired through the breach. Sting is necessary but not sufficient.

**Anti-precog immunity does NOT counter the Simurgh.** She has pretercognition (precog + postcog), telekinesis, psychic scream (Master), and tinker-copying. Anti-precog blocks one sense; the others remain.

## Matchup writeups

### Zion (The Warrior Entity)

**Canon**: End-of-cycle entity. Kills Endbringers. Reality-warper. Has PtV-equivalent (his own path-to-victory). Hardcoded blindspots: scion/Entities, Endbringers, Eidolon, trigger events.

**Counters** (CYOA-faithful):
- **Trump stack** (FQ + Oberon + Eidolon + PM): Eidolon-via-Tier-2 makes you a precog blindspot to Zion. FQ can touch-store him if you can get to him. PM can copy Entity powers.
- **Sting Tier 1 (25 SP)**: Breaches his avatar-Entity portal (like canon).
- **Tinker superweapon**: Required in canon. CYOA doesn't have a single "Tinker superweapon" pick, but you can stack multiple Tinker-class picks.

**Weakness**: Susceptible to Trump-class powers (his own blindspot includes Eidolon, who is Trump-class).

**Verdict**: Trump stack + Sting + All Seeing Precognition perk is the canon-faithful answer. Without those, you lose.

### Eden (The Thinker Entity)

**Canon**: Crashed into a planet. Shards distributed. In this CYOA, she never died (Difficulty = Suicide/Det Mode: "Eden never died. You cannot take the options that instakill the Entities or Endbringers.").

**Counters**: Same as Zion. Note: under Suicide/Det Mode, **instakill options on Entities are FORBIDDEN**. Under Easy/Normal difficulty, no such restriction.

**Verdict**: Easy mode + Trump stack = full clear (instakill permitted). Suicide/Det mode + Trump stack = containment, not kill.

### The Simurgh

**Canon**: Pretercognition (precog + postcog combined), telekinesis, psychic scream (Master), tinker-copying, "blind in the present." She plans in years. Builds bombs that trigger years later. Resistant to most direct counters.

**Counters**:
- **Anti-precog does NOT counter her** (she has postcognition, TK, scream).
- **Cloak Mark III** (3 SP + 2 CP): "blind spot to Endbringer and Entity precognitives" — DOES work against Simurgh precog.
- **Anti-Master** (Mental Barrier, Monologue drawback, etc.) — for her scream.
- **Stranger-stacking** — she can't see you in the present anyway.
- **PtV with All Seeing Precognition** — but PtV can't see Entities/Endbringers, so even with blindspots cleared, the Simurgh's pretercognition is non-standard.

**Weakness**: "Blind in the present" — she perceives only past and future. Take her by surprise in the immediate moment. Cloak Mark III is the best defense against her precog specifically.

**Verdict**: She is a meta-threat, not a Trump-specific one. Stack Master-immunity + Cloak III + Stranger effects. Without those, she's a slow-burn TKO you can't fully prevent.

### Scion (Zion's avatar)

**Canon**: Golden light beams, flight, force field, his own path-to-victory. KILLABLE but only via specific multi-step method.

**The kill chain** (from canon, verified):
1. **Psychological warfare**: Oliver's power made Scion briefly think he saw Eden, then heroes crushed that hope. Scion stopped using PtV to defend.
2. **Sting-infused bolts**: Foil imbued two bolts with Sting; Ballistic fired them into Scion's avatar. Sting "wedged open" the portal between avatar and main body.
3. **Tinker superweapon**: A collaborative Tinker weapon fired through the opened portal, destroyed the main body.

**Counters** (CYOA-faithful):
- **(Blaster/Striker) Sting (Tier 1, 25 SP)**: "infuse anything you can touch, any projectile you fire, or any power you manifest" — broadest Sting variant.
- **Flechette/Foil (Power Copy, 10 SP)**: Sting on nonliving objects only.
- **Eidolon-via-Tier-2** with anti-Entity loadout.
- **(Brute) Endbringer** (Tier 2, 18 SP) — for the "Behemoth-style self-kaboom" if desperate.

**Verdict**: Sting + Tinker superweapon + psychological warfare is the canon answer. The CYOA gives you Sting (Sting Tier 1 + Flechette) and Trump-stack access to Eidolon's anti-Entity loadout. The psychological warfare step is hard to engineer in-game; CYOA doesn't address it directly.

### Contessa (The bogeyman)

**Canon**: PtV with hardcoded blindspots (Entities, Endbringers, Eidolon, trigger events). Doctor Mother.

**Counters**:
- **Fairy Queen (Tier 2, 25 SP)**: Touch-kill + store. But she's hard to touch (PtV plans around touch attacks).
- **Eidolon-via-Tier-2**: You BECOME a precognitive blindspot. She can't see you directly.
- **All Seeing Precognition perk** (20 SP + 10 CP): Removes your own PtV blindspots. (Different mechanism — makes your PtV see everything, doesn't make her PtV see you.)

**Weakness**: She needs to physically approach you for FQ. Eidolon (Tier 2) makes you invisible to her PtV, so she can't path a touch-attack against you.

**Verdict**: Eidolon (Tier 2) + Eidolon-prevents-her-seeing-you + FQ if you can corner her. Plus Determination Mode has "Contessa is still alive with her restricted power" — likely means Cloak-style immunity extends to her PtV.

### The Endbringers

**Canon**: Cycle-aligned. Behemoth (dynakinesis), Leviathan (hydrokinesis), Simurgh (psychic), Tohu/Bohu (variable). During Gold Morning, the Endbringers fought Scion *alongside* capes — the Simurgh voluntarily worked with Tattletale, etc. They were NOT mind-controlled by Khepri.

**Counters**:
- **The End-Becomer (Combo, 10 SP)**: "You can take control over any single one of the existing Endbringers, while keeping all your other powers as well as getting theirs." — CYOA invention; directly controls an Endbringer.
- **(Master) Khepri (Tier 3, 15 SP)**: Controls capes (not Endbringers in canon, but CYOA doesn't make this distinction clear).
- **(Brute) Endbringer (Tier 2, 18 SP)**: Become an Endbringer-body. **Downside: if your core is destroyed, you die.**
- **Flechette/Foil (Power Copy, 10 SP) + group fire**: Canon-answer; works in CYOA.
- **The End-Becomer + Eidolon + Flechette = 53 SP** for full neutralization.

**Verdict**: End-Becomer (Combo, 10 SP) is the most cost-efficient Endbringer answer.

### Eidolon (canon character)

**Canon**: 3 powers, swappable. Created Endbringers as "worthy opponents" (Interlude 27b). Wildbow WoG: his power-drain came from being "hooked up to three (later five) giant energy drains" — the Endbringers themselves. So the "worthy opponent" issue and the power-drain are linked.

**Counters**:
- **FQ Eidolon in-world** (need Tier 2 Fairy Queen at 25 SP).
- **Eidolon-via-Tier-2** makes you a precog blindspot AND removes the worthy-opponent issue (so you don't have to deal with Endbringer-drain either).
- **Bait-and-switch**: FQ him and store his 3 powers in FQ slots.

**Verdict**: FQ him. He's a 25-SP power battery. Tier 2 Eidolon is also viable as your own power.

### Faerie Queen (Glaistig Uaine, canon)

**Canon**: Can claim parahumans who die in her vicinity (passive). Can touch-kill to actively claim. Maintains 3 shades at full power, more at reduced strength.

**Counters**:
- **FQ-of-FQ recursion** (per §1.0(2) most-generous reading): Your FQ kills her, you store her FQ. Your FQ now has 3 FQ-shades, each with 3 slots = 9 stored capes.
- **Khepri (Tier 3)** for body control.
- **Stranger** to avoid her touch (Imp, Nice Guy).
- **Trumped the Trump (Perk, 3 SP)**: Her FQ can't steal from you.

**Verdict**: FQ her. Trumped the Trump (3 SP) prevents her stealing you. Khepri (Tier 3) controls her body.

### Number Man (combat Thinker)

**Canon**: Perfect math. Sees patterns. Plans perfectly. Limited to math, not omniscience.

**Counters**:
- **Random Master effects** (Cherish 10 SP Power Copy, Regent 7 SP Power Copy) — break his math by introducing non-mathematical effects.
- **Bugs (Skitter/Weaver, 6 SP Power Copy)** — overwhelming volume breaks pattern recognition.
- **Brute physical overwhelm** (Lung 9 SP, Aegis 4 SP, Bitch 4 SP).

**Verdict**: Easy win with any Brute + surprise attack.

### Slaughterhouse 9

**Canon**: Cherish, Crawler, Bonesaw, Siberian, Jack Slash, Mannequin, Burnscar, Shatterbird, plus Hatchet Face.

**Counters**:
- **Khepri (Tier 3, 15 SP)** controls all of them within 10m. She can kill Siberian (Manton-limited, but Khepri controls her anyway).
- **Bonesaw turned**: She has a conscience; can be reasoned with.
- **Siberian is Manton-limited** — her projection doesn't affect living things directly. But Khepri control bypasses this.

**Verdict**: Khepri (Tier 3, 15 SP) alone wins. 15 SP. Done.

### Khepri (canon, post-GM)

**Canon**: Taylor after Panacea's Corona Pollentia modification. Controls capes via QA. Range extended via Doormaker portals + Clairvoyant targeting. Controlled 5,212 capes during Gold Morning. Could NOT control Sleeper, Teacher, Contessa, dead capes, Gray Boy loops, time bubbles. **Did NOT control Endbringers** (they're shard-constructs without Coronas).

**Counters**:
- **Bug-resistant materials** (fire, sealed suits).
- **Master her first** (she has Master immunity for her control but can be controlled herself).
- **Khepri-via-Tier-3 (15 SP)** on your side: Mirror match. Whoever controls the other first wins.
- **Endbringer-class durability** to survive her Master effect.

**Verdict**: You having Khepri yourself (Tier 3, 15 SP) is the best counter. Range is 10m, so you need to get close.

| Eden | Same as Zion, plus instakill if Easy mode |
| Simurgh | **Not a Trump question** — Stack Cloak III + Master-immunity + Stranger |

| Contessa | Eidolon (you're invisible to her PtV) + FQ (touch-steal) |

| Number Man | Brute + surprise |
| S9 | Khepri (15 SP Tier 3) |

**Recommended Trump-stack budget**:
- **Tier 1 (1 pick)**: Oberon/Titania (35 SP) — 5 active copies
- **Tier 2 (3 picks)**: Eidolon (20) + Fairy Queen (25) + Power Manipulator (20) = 65 SP
- **Tier 3 (5 picks)**: Power Sight (4) + Powerswap (4) + Fae Court (6) + Khepri (15) + (Breaker) Phase (2) = 31 SP
- **Combo (5 picks)**: Path to Victory (15) + Legion of the Fae (15) + End-Becomer (10) + Meta Knowledge (5) + open = 45 SP
- **Perks**: Trumped the Trump (3) + All Seeing Precognition (20 SP + 10 CP)
- **Power Copy (1 pick)**: Coil (7 SP) — backup timeline
- **Total**: ~206 SP + 10 CP

Difficulty: Determination Mode (+100 SP) + 5 best drawbacks (~+100 SP) + Warrior Entity (no Shard of Thinker to avoid Cauldron debt) = **~200 SP starting budget**. The full Trump stack fits comfortably.
