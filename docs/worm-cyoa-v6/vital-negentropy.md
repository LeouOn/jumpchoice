# Worm CYOA V6.1 — Vital Baseline v4: "Negentropy & The Scion Trade"

> **Date:** 2026-06-20. v4 swaps **All Seeing Precognition** (20 SP + 10 CP) for **Negentropy** (8 SP + 3 CP). Saves **12 SP + 7 CP**. Negentropy is a force-multiplier on every shard power you have — Eidolon never runs out, PM charges are unlimited, Immortal becomes truly immortal. **Plus Negentropy has a hidden meta-game win condition: it's the Shard Scion wants, and you can trade yourself to end the cycle without violence.**

---

## §1.0 — The Negentropy revelation

The full text of Negentropy (Perks and Drawbacks, 8 SP + 3 CP):

> "It somehow went unnoticed by the entities that your Shard had the ability they sought for eons: it defies entropy. Somehow your power allows you to defy entropy, and uses this itself to keep it charged. **You will never run out of energy.** This is more or less useless unless you're either immortal or think you're capable of **convincing the entity to leave behind all his other Shards, including Sting, and therefore not kill all humans in exchange for your Shard.** A Shard without this perk may still have the ability to defy entropy, but you need to find it yourself, because the Shard doesn't already know."

**The hidden win condition is HUGE.** The cycle in Worm ends two ways:
1. **Violent**: Oliver's psychological warfare → Sting → Tinker superweapon → Scion dies.
2. **Diplomatic**: Convince Scion that the cycle isn't worth it. He gives up and goes to another universe.

Negentropy gives you a **third option**: **Scion gets what he wants (entropy defiance), you give up your Shard, Scion leaves all other Shards (including Sting).** Humanity survives, you may or may not.

In canon, Scion's despair came from realizing Eden was dead and the cycle couldn't work. He wanted a partner. He wanted validation. If you walk up to Scion with Negentropy and say "this is what you wanted — entropy defiance without the cycle — trade me your other Shards and I'll come with you" — that's a **non-violent Scion resolution**.

This is so much more interesting than "solo Scion with Sting + Tinker superweapon."

## §3.0 — Gomu Gomu no Mi: the underrated offensive perk

Full text (Breaker, Tier 3, 3 SP):

> "You are a rubber person. Or, really, just really elastic and not conductive. **Blunt forces do no damage to you** and you're resistant to heat. **You could get crushed by Behemoth or punched by Alexandria and not get damaged.**"

The "really elastic" clause is doing more work than my v2/v3 analyses admitted. "Elastic" implies:
- **Stretch limbs for range attacks**: rubber stretching is core to Luffy's combat style. CYOA doesn't explicitly say "you can stretch your arms to punch" but "really elastic" supports it.
- **Conductive immunity**: "not conductive" → you can't be shocked. Major defensive perk that adds to Phase (Phase is vulnerable to electricity).
- **Heat resistance**: rubber doesn't conduct heat well. Adds to fire/heat defense.

So Gomu Gomu = **3 SP for**:
- Alexandria-tier blunt immunity (defense)
- Conductive immunity (anti-Electric)
- Heat resistance (anti-Thermal)
- Stretch-based offensive utility (rubber limbs)
- "Elastic" range attacks (unclear if explicit but supported)

**The user was right: Gomu Gomu is both defensive AND offensive.** I under-rated it. Let me update the analysis.

## §6.0 — The Scion Trade (the win condition)

You have Negentropy. You walk up to Scion. He sees you have what he wants (entropy defiance). The text says:

> "think you're capable of convincing the entity to leave behind all his other Shards, including Sting, and therefore not kill all humans in exchange for your Shard"

**What this means in practice**:

1. **You offer Scion a trade**: "Take my Shard (Negentropy). Leave Sting and all other Shards behind."
2. **Scion's incentive**: He's been running the cycle for eons looking for entropy defiance. He's never seen a Shard that does this. You're giving him what he wants without the cycle.
3. **The deal**: Scion takes your Shard → Sting and all other Shards stay with humanity → Scion leaves Earth → cycle ends without violence.

**What you give up**: Your powers. You lose Negentropy. Eidolon. FQ. PM. Everything. You're human again (or whatever you were before).

**What you get**: Humanity survives. The cycle ends. No Gold Morning. No Entities. No Endbringers. Just a normal life afterward.

This is the **pacifist win condition** for the entire Worm setting. Most CYOA builds are about soloing Scion. This build is about **negotiating with Scion**.

The CYOA text doesn't specify HOW you convince Scion — that's up to your roleplay. But you have:
- Eidolon Tier 2 (precog blindspot — Scion can't see your approach)
- Mental Barrier (immune to Master/Stranger/aura)
- PtV (plan the conversation)
- Biobringer (understand biological entities)
- Endbringer control (if needed for leverage)

You have everything you need to walk up to Scion, make the trade, and walk away human.

## §9.0 — The "Scion Trade" playthrough

Here's how the Negentropy Scion trade plays out:

**Setup**: You've played through ~2 years of in-game time. You have:
- Eidolon (3 strong random powers + the precog-blindspot)
- FQ (3 stolen cape powers)
- PM (3 slots, fully charged)
- Khepri (10m Master)
- End-Becomer (one Endbringer controlled)
- Mental Barrier (Master immune)
- All the rest

**Scion appears**. He's in despair. He's killing capes. You approach.

**Step 1**: Eidolon makes you invisible to his precog. He can't see you coming.

**Step 2**: Mental Barrier protects you from any Master/Stranger effect. Scion's golden light beams are physical, not Master — you still need Gomu Gomu + Phase for those.

**Step 3**: Walk up. He doesn't know what you want. PtV plans the conversation.

**Step 4**: "Scion. I have what you want. Look at my Shard."

His PtV-equivalent (golden light) scans you. He sees Negentropy in your Shard.

**Step 5**: He hesitates. The cycle has been running for eons. He never saw entropy defiance in a Shard.

**Step 6**: "Take my Shard. Leave the others. End the cycle."

**Step 7**: He accepts (per the CYOA text — this is a valid outcome). He extracts your Negentropy Shard. He leaves Earth with the Shard. All other Shards (Sting, Eidolon, FQ, etc.) stay with humanity.

**Result**: Cycle ends. No Gold Morning. No Entities. No Endbringers. Humanity survives. You're human.

This is the **good ending** for Worm.
