# Worm CYOA V6.1 — The Drawback Catalog: "Baddies Attack Me" Edition

> **Date:** 2026-06-20. A systematic audit of every drawback that gives budget in exchange for either (a) enemies attacking you, or (b) other inconveniences. Tailored for self-insert players (no parents to lose). Ranks each drawback by **net budget gain vs. severity** so you can pick the right combo.

---

## §1.0 — Methodology

Drawbacks are sorted by category:
- **Tier S (must-take)**: +30+ SP/CP gain AND the "downside" is actually a buff for a Trump-stack build
- **Tier A (great value)**: +20-30 SP/CP gain, manageable downside
- **Tier B (situational)**: +10-20 SP/CP gain, depends on build
- **Tier C (niche)**: +5-15 SP/CP gain, roleplay-only
- **Tier D (trap)**: Any gain, downside cripples the build
- **Skipped**: Orphaned (you said no), Perks like Wealthy (cost CP, not drawbacks)

## §2.0 — "Baddies Attack Me" drawbacks (your favorite category)

### **Tier S — Must-take**

#### **Golden Avatar** (-23 SP -7 CP = +30 SP/CP)
> "The Warrior Entity is trapped in his avatar. He is still capable of using all his powers, to some degree or another, but he cannot disable the facsimile of human emotions he feels, and destroying the Avatar will kill him permanently. He is stuck fighting on the level he was at during Gold Morning."

**Why it's Tier S**: This is THE centerpiece drawback. Scion is:
- Locked in avatar (can't escape to space)
- At Gold Morning power level (weaker than canon full-power Scion)
- Has emotions (can't disable them — you can manipulate him)
- **Avatar death = permanent death**

For a Trump-stack build, this is the key to both diplomatic (Scion Trade via Negentropy) AND violent (solo Scion) resolutions. +30 SP/CP for a buff masquerading as a drawback.

**Verdict**: Take this. Always. There's no downside for a Trump-stack player.

---

#### **Enraged Avatar** (-15 SP -15 CP = +30 SP/CP)
> "The Warrior Entity is trapped in his avatar. He is still capable of using all his powers to their full strength, but he cannot disable the facsimile of human emotions he feels, and destroying the Avatar will kill him permanently."

**Why it's Tier S**: Same as Golden Avatar but Scion keeps full power. Less safe but still avatar-locked + permanent death.

**Verdict**: Take Golden Avatar instead. Strictly better (weaker Scion, same gain).

### **Tier A — Great value**

#### **May you live in Interesting Times** (-20 SP -20 CP = +40 SP/CP)
> "You attract trouble. Even if you try to live a completely mundane life, a strong Parahuman will always find you and you'll never go without a challenging fight. **This could be considered a Perk if you have a power that grows with use** or that gets significantly more useful with increased skill, but it ensures you're never overpowered."

**Why it's Tier A**: The text literally says "This could be considered a Perk." For a Trump-stack build where FQ/Eidolon/PM scale with use, **constant fights = constant steals/copies/RNGs**. You want enemies to attack.

**Verdict**: +40 SP/CP for a buff. Take it.

---

#### **Second Wave Parahumans** (-30 SP -10 CP = +40 SP/CP)
> "A wave of triggers ripples across the globe (or galaxy if you're in a space setting), even among those who had no potential to become Parahumans prior to the ripple. The ripple has a lesser threshold than a normal Trigger, closer to that of a Second Generation Trigger, though these Parahumans can still second trigger. All these Parahumans have Shar..."

**Why it's Tier A**: Creates a wave of new capes worldwide. For a Trump-stack, **new capes = new FQ targets, new Eidolon inspirations, new PM copy opportunities**. The world gets more dangerous, but you're the one who benefits from more capes.

**Verdict**: +40 SP/CP and the world gets more targets. Take it.

---

#### **Late to the Party** (-20 SP -5 CP = +25 SP/CP)
> "One good person, one neutral person, and one evil person arrive in the same universe as you with a build of their choice, except that they arrive at a time before you, don't arrive at the same time as each other, and have the same Shard Ranking as you."

**Why it's Tier A**: Three rival self-inserts with their own builds. They could be allies or enemies depending on alignment. **Three other min-maxed capes** in your world.

**Verdict**: +25 SP/CP and three rivals to either recruit, defeat, or avoid. Good for narrative tension. Take it if you want a challenge.

### **Tier B — Situational**

#### **Slaughterhouse 8** (-5 SP -10 CP = +15 SP/CP)
> "If the Slaughterhouse 9 has formed, they're after you. Not to kill you or torture you, no, something worse. **They have a position open and they want to recruit you.** If the Slaughterhouse 9 has yet to form, they will, and they will survive long enough to try to recruit you."

**Why it's Tier B**: S9 wants to recruit you. They're persistent, powerful, and led by Jack Slash. For a Trump-stack with Trumped the Trump, Jack Slash's broadcast-Trump doesn't work on you. **Khepri (Tier 3) controls all of S9 at once within 10m.**

**Why not Tier A**: +15 SP/CP is modest. S9 includes Bonesaw (biotinker), Siberian (Manton projection), Crawler (not pickable in CYOA, but exists in-world), and Jack Slash. They're persistent. You can defeat them with Khepri + Trumped the Trump but it's an ongoing threat.

**Verdict**: Take if you want S9 storyline. Manageable with Trumped the Trump + Khepri. **+15 SP/CP is fine but not great.**

---

#### **Endbringer Target** (-10 SP -5 CP = +15 SP/CP)
> "The first time you go to an Endbringer fight, the Endbringer will go slightly out of its way to kill you. Unless you show that you're very powerful, you won't be its main focus."

**Why it's Tier B**: First Endbringer fight, the Endbringer focuses on you specifically. Manageable if you have Gomu Gomu + Phase + Mental Barrier (anti-attack, anti-aura, anti-Master).

**Why not Tier A**: +15 SP/CP is modest. Surviving one Endbringer fight with focus on you is risky.

**Verdict**: Take if you have strong durability (Gomu Gomu + Phase + Immortal). Skip if you don't.

---

#### **Fallen Worship** (-5 SP -10 CP = +15 SP/CP)
> "The Fallen believe you to be one of the Endbringers, possibly not believing you've yet to receive whatever divine wisdom causes the Endbringers to destroy cities every couple of months. They will worship you and try to get you to act like the Endbringers. Even if you don't take this, they might still come to this conclusion."

**Why it's Tier B**: The Fallen are a cult that worships Endbringers. They'll worship you and try to manipulate you into Endbringer-like behavior. **This is a social drawback, not a combat one.** The Fallen are mostly low-tier capes.

**Why not Tier A**: +15 SP/CP. The Fallen are annoying but not endgame threats.

**Verdict**: Take if you want Fallen storyline. The Fallen are weak capes you can FQ/Eidolon/PM easily. **+15 SP/CP.**

## §3.0 — High-budget non-combat drawbacks

### **Tier A — Must-take for Trump-stack**

#### **Overcharge Strike** (-30 SP = +30 SP)
> "I lack the creativity that humans have... which is why I'm bribing you with awesome powers to kill the entity for me. But if you find the idea of killing an Entity too daunting, **I could just overcharge your Shard... to locate, target, and destroy all Shards necessary for the entity to maintain a consciousness, effectively putting the entity in a permanent comatose state.** I'll make sure that the corpse comes to the attention of someone who can stop the entity's corpse from being resurrected. Your Shard will be permanently crippled and will have a fraction of the room for powers."

**Why it's Tier A**: The Entity (Last One, the CYOA author-insert) **puts Scion in a permanent coma for you.** Scion is neutralized. No fight needed.

**Downside**: Your Shard is "permanently crippled" with "less room for powers" — this is the 30 SP cost. You lose flexibility but keep your chosen powers.

**Verdict**: If you want to skip the Scion fight entirely, take this. **+30 SP for an Entity-coma win condition.**

---

### **Tier A — Strong budget, manageable downside**

#### **Insane** (-20 SP +15 CP = +5 net)
> "You hallucinate and cannot differentiate your imagination from reality. You have moments of clarity. **Your powers get stronger the lower your clarity is at any point in time. At your worst, your powers are twice as powerful.** At your best, your powers are slightly stronger than described."

**Why it's Tier A**: Powers are **2x stronger at worst clarity**. Eidolon at 2x strength. PM at 2x strength. FQ ghosts at 2x strength. **This is a buff disguised as a drawback.**

**Downside**: You hallucinate. Can't differentiate imagination from reality. Mental Barrier doesn't help (it's not Master). Noctis Cape (eidetic memory) might help anchor you.

**Verdict**: Risky but the 2x power buff is enormous. Take if you can manage the hallucinations. +5 SP/CP net is weak but the buff is real.

---

#### **Corona Pollentia** (-15 SP -15 CP = +30 SP/CP)
> "You have yet to trigger with your powers. To trigger you will need to get deeply traumatized. There is a possibility you will trigger from finding out you fell through a portal in your sleep into Earth Bet and that if you don't fix things, the whole multiverse, including your own Earth, will be destroyed, or at least heavily damaged."

**Why it's Tier A**: +30 SP/CP. You start without powers but can trigger from the meta-knowledge of "the multiverse will be destroyed." For a self-insert, this is basically free.

**Downside**: You're powerless until you trigger. Self-inserts can trigger immediately from the "oh god I'm in Worm and everything dies" realization.

**Verdict**: +30 SP/CP for self-inserts. Take it.

**Why it's Tier B**: +15 SP/CP. For self-inserts, you forget your past life but keep Worm knowledge. **This is actually useful for a self-insert** — you have canon knowledge without emotional baggage.

**Verdict**: Take for self-inserts. +15 SP/CP and you keep the meta-knowledge.

**Why it's Tier B**: +13 SP/CP. You look monstrous. For self-inserts, you can choose the appearance, so you can pick "looks like an elf" or "looks slightly off" instead of full Case 53 monstrous.

## §4.0 — "Used to give more, now doesn't" — what changed

You noted that some drawbacks "used to give more points" but now don't. Without access to prior CYOA versions (V5, V6.0), I can't verify exact changes. But the pattern in V6.1 suggests:

**Drawbacks that feel undercosted in V6.1:**
- **Slaughterhouse 8** (+15 SP/CP) — S9 is endgame-level threat. +15 feels low.
- **Endbringer Target** (+15 SP/CP) — Endbringer focus is death sentence for low-tier. +15 feels low for the risk.
- **Fallen Worship** (+15 SP/CP) — The Fallen are annoying but not endgame. +15 feels right.
- **Wanted** (+5 CP) — Tiny gain for ongoing social friction. Feels undercosted.

**Drawbacks that feel overcosted (great value):**
- **Golden Avatar** (+30 SP/CP) — The "downside" is a buff for Trump-stacks.
- **May you live in Interesting Times** (+40 SP/CP) — The "downside" is a buff for scaling builds.
- **Second Wave Parahumans** (+40 SP/CP) — The "downside" creates more FQ targets.
- **Corona Pollentia** (+30 SP/CP) — For self-inserts, this is nearly free.
- **Overcharge Strike** (+30 SP) — Entity-coma is a win condition.

The V6.1 balance seems to reward "meta-game drawbacks" (Avatar-locking, Interesting Times, Second Wave) more than "direct attack" drawbacks (S8, Endbringer Target). This makes sense from a game-design perspective: the meta-game drawbacks are scarier for new players but trivial for min-maxers.

## §6.0 — The S8 Simulation Scenario

You mentioned wanting to play the S8 scenario. Here's how it plays out with the v5 build:

### Setup

You're a self-insert in Brockton Bay, 2011. You have the v5 Vital Baseline build (FQ + Eidolon + PM + Khepri + Gomu Gomu + Phase + Trumped the Trump + Mental Barrier + Cloak IV + Noctis Cape + Target Acquired + Negentropy + Golden Avatar + Plot Armor). You took Slaughterhouse 8 (+15 SP/CP).

The S9 has formed. Jack Slash, Bonesaw, Siberian, Crawler (in-world, not pickable), Burnscar, Mannequin, Shatterbird, Cherish, Hatchet Face. They've heard about you. They're coming to recruit you.

### The S9 roster vs. your build

| S9 member | Their threat | Your counter |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Jack Slash** | Broadcast-Trump (senses capes' emotional states, manipulates conflicts) | **Trumped the Trump (3 SP)** — his Broadcast doesn't work on you. You're invisible to his Trump-sense. |
| **Bonesaw** | Biotinker (plagues, modifications, pain bugs) | **Mental Barrier** — immune to her Master effects. **Biobringer** — you control carbon-based life better than she does. |
| **Siberian** | Manton projection (invulnerable, splits matter) | **Khepri (10m Master)** — control Manton (the projector). Or **Phase** — her projection can't touch your gaseous form. Or find Manton and FQ him. |
| **Crawler** | Adaptive regeneration | **Gomu Gomu** — his blunt attacks don't hurt you. **FQ** — touch-kill, steal his regeneration. |
| **Burnscar** | Fire/teleportation | **Gomu Gomu** — heat resistant. **Phase** — her fire passes through your gaseous form. |
| **Mannequin** | Tinker (sealed suit, weapons) | **Target Acquired** — see him through walls. **Phase** — his weapons pass through you. |
| **Shatterbird** | Silicon control (glass) | **Phase** — her glass passes through you. **Gomu Gomu** — blunt glass doesn't hurt. |
| **Cherish** | Emotion read/control | **Mental Barrier** — full immunity. **Cloak Mark IV** — she can't info-derive you. |
| **Hatchet Face** | Power-nullifier | **Trumped the Trump** — his power-nullification is a Trump effect. It doesn't work on you. |

### The encounter

**Jack Slash approaches first.** He tries his Broadcast-Trump to read your emotional state. **It doesn't work** (Trumped the Trump). He's confused. He tries his blade-extension Blaster power. **Phase** — his blades pass through your gaseous form.

**He monologues** (you both have Monologue, so you both monologue). You offer him a trade: "Walk away. I'm not joining." He refuses. He signals the S9.

**Bonesaw deploys a bioplague.** Mental Barrier protects you from the Master component. Biobringer lets you control the plague itself — you redirect it back at the S9.

**Siberian charges.** Phase — she passes through you. You use Power Sight to identify Manton (the projector is nearby, always is). Khepri within 10m — control Manton. Siberian vanishes.

**Crawler rampages.** Gomu Gomu — his blunt attacks don't hurt. You close to touch range. **FQ touch-kill.** Crawler dies. You store his regeneration in FQ slot 1.

**Burnscar, Mannequin, Shatterbird** — Phase handles all of them. Their attacks pass through you.

**Cherish** — tries emotion control. Mental Barrier = full immunity. She panics.

**Hatchet Face** — tries power-nullification. Trumped the Trump = doesn't work. He's confused. Khepri within 10m — control him.

**Jack Slash** — last one standing. He tries to negotiate. You FQ him. He dies. You store his Broadcast-Trump in FQ slot 2.

### Result

**S9 defeated in one encounter.** You now have:
- FQ slot 1: Crawler's regeneration
- FQ slot 2: Jack Slash's Broadcast-Trump
- FQ slot 3: open (save for Eidolon or Bonesaw)
- Legion of the Fae: 50% copies of all 9 S9 members

**The S8 drawback gave you +15 SP/CP and 9 new FQ/Legion targets.** This is why "baddies attack me" drawbacks are good for Trump-stacks: **every attacker becomes a new power source.**
