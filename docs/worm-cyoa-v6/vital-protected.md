# Worm CYOA V6.1 — Vital Baseline v3: "Protected, not Paranoid"

> **Date:** 2026-06-20. Revised from the original Vital Baseline. **Drops All Seeing Precognition (20 SP + 10 CP) and Cloak Mark II (2 SP + 3 CP)** — they're "convenience" perks, not "instant game over" protections. **Adds Mental Barrier (1 SP + 5 CP) — full Master + Stranger immunity + Victoria aura immunity.** Keeps Trumped the Trump (anti-Trump-stack), Gomu Gomu no Mi (Alexandria blunt), Phase (bullet/laser), Noctis Cape (no sleep), Target Acquired (anti-Stranger radar), Cloak Mark IV (anti-info-Thinker).

---

## §1.0 — Philosophy of protection

The original Vital Baseline had four overlapping "hidden from precogs" perks. **That's overkill for most threats.** This revision follows a stricter rule:

**A perk earns its slot if and only if it prevents an instant-loss scenario.**

Three instant-loss categories exist in Worm:
1. **Trump-stack steal/copy** — Eidolon/FQ/PM strip your powers → Trumped the Trump (3 SP) hard-counters this.
2. **Physical insta-kill** — Alexandria punch, Behemoth stomp, Flechette Sting on corneas → Gomu Gomu no Mi (3 SP) + Phase (2 SP) cover blunt and ranged.
3. **Mind control / stealth** — Cherish, Regent, Khepri, Imp → Mental Barrier (1 SP + 5 CP) is full immunity.

Anything else is convenience. We're cutting convenience.

## §7.0 — The honest trade-off

You lose:
- **PtV blindspot removal** (20 SP + 10 CP). PtV still works for everything NOT in its blindspots: capes, logistics, weakpoints, conversations, social engineering. You just can't auto-path Eidolon-class threats or Entities.

You gain:
- **Full Master/Stranger immunity** including Victoria aura. Mental Barrier is better than Cloak IV for this.
- **Biobringer** (10 SP Combo) — carbon-based life control. Synergizes with Khepri.
- **Target Acquired** (2 SP Tier 3) — anti-Stranger radar at 300m.
- **17 CP for skills/identity** (vs. 10 CP original).

For a player who wants to **survive threats and have headroom** rather than **path through meta-threats**, this is the better build.

For a player who specifically wants to **solo Scion** or **auto-path Entities**, you need ASP back, which costs combo/perk slots.

## §8.0 — When to add ASP back

Add ASP if:
- You're going to fight Entities directly (Gold Morning, solo Scion)
- You want PtV to be a "win button" rather than a "plan most things" tool
- You're willing to drop Biobringer + Target Acquired + 1 Combo to fit it

Keep this build if:
- You want to survive most threats with counters, not overpower them
- You want the skill/identity headroom for character flavor
- You're OK with PtV's blindspots (you can use Eidolon to RNG around them via PtV's "what to slot" question)

**My recommendation for "I want to play this character":** keep this build. It's the most well-rounded Vital baseline.

**My recommendation for "I want to solo Scion":** swap in ASP, drop Biobringer + Target Acquired, keep Mental Barrier. You'll have PtV with no blindspots but lose Biobringer and anti-Stranger radar.
