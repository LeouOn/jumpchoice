## The Fairy Queen kill sequence (corrected)

1. **Find a cape** with Power Sight (4 SP). See what they have.
2. **Engage safely**. FQ requires a *touch* kill. If they have Master powers, prep Master immunity or stay 30m+ away.
3. **Touch kill**. The cape dies. Their shard is captured.
4. **Choose to store or release**. Stored = you can summon their power at will. Released = the power goes to the next available host.
5. **You have 3 active slots**. Use them for high-impact capes only:
   - **Eidolon** (3 random powers, but Eidolon himself is captured, so his '3 random' is now 3 you control)
   - **Contessa** (Path to Victory, with blindspots)
   - **Doormaker** (portals)
   - **Glaistig Uaine** (her own FQ — recursion; see below)
   - **Legend** (best blaster in canon)
6. **OR scavenge corpses.** FQ text says *"You can steal their power from their corpse"* — so you don't need to be the killer. Battlefields and aftermaths are FQ-fodder.
7. **Oberon/Titania (35 SP) lets you touch-copy on top of FQ's storage**. Use Oberon in a room full of capes, then FQ-kill the strongest one for 4 total power sets (3 FQ slots + 1 Oberon copy).

## The Eidolon trick (use him right — corrected)

Eidolon's text: *"You have Eidolon's powers without the worthy opponent issue. You can have three somewhat-random powers that you can change at any time you want... You can switch out quality for quantity and have five weaker powers instead of three stronger ones. All powers take a couple of minutes to build up strength... You're a precognitive blindspot to everyone but the entity."*

- Start with 3 random powers. Swap to find a useful trio. No limit specified, so likely unlimited rerolls.
- **You are a precognitive blindspot** — this is the killer defensive feature. PtV, Contessa, Dinah, Tattletale's info-derivation cannot target you directly.
- Pair with FQ: store Eidolon himself. Now you have FQ's 3 slots + Eidolon's 3 slots + your own kit = **9 powers min.**

## The Oberon/Titania lock (corrected)

Oberon's text: *"You can gain a permanent copy of the power of any Parahuman you touch. This works on the substance in Cauldron Vials and the Garden of Eden too, though adapted for human use. You can only have five of these powers active at a time, but rather than the Fairy Queen's ghosts, you have these powers for yourself."*

- **Touch** = physical contact. Standard English. Plan to get within arm of reach.
- **Permanent** = no time limit. Once copied, it's yours forever (until you swap).
- **Copy** = duplicate. The original cape keeps their power (you're not stealing).
- **Any Parahuman** = no class restriction. Brute, Master, Trump, Thinker, all.
- **5-active cap** — you can have unlimited capes copied but only 5 active simultaneously. Swap by dismissing.
- **Cauldron + Eden clause** — copy powers from Cauldron vials and Eden's own superweapons. This means you can grab powers from formula-derived capes (Cauldron capes) and Eden's own garden.

## The recursive fairy queen problem

If you have FQ and use FQ to kill+store another FQ, you now have FQ-FQ. That FQ-FQ can kill and store other capes. You have 3 FQ slots, so you could store 3 FQs, each with 3 slots = 9 stored capes. **And each of those FQs can use their slots independently**.

**Theoretical cap**: 3 FQs × 3 slots each = 9 stored capes + your base kit + Eidolon's 3 + Oberon's 5 + PM's 3 = **23 power sets min.** But this is purely theoretical.

**Cycle limit**: The CYOA doesn't cap FQ recursion. Read the text: *"With a touch you can kill a Parahuman and steal their power. You can store up to three."* The word 'Parahuman' could exclude the FQ-stored (since they're ghosts, not capes). Per §1.0(2) most-generous-reading, **recursion is allowed**; per strict-reading, **recursion is questionable**.
