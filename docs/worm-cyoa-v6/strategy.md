## Currency & Budget Math

Two currencies, both start at 0:
- **Shard Points (SP)** — spent on Powers (Tier 1/2/3, Power Copy, Combo) and Difficulty
- **Character Points (CP)** — spent on Skills, Identity, Location, Alignment, Age multipliers, etc.

### Budget levers, ranked by efficiency

**Free / drawback money (no SP cost, gain points or stuff):**
- Difficulty = Easy: **+30 SP** (the easiest money in the build, no strings)
- Difficulty = Death Mode: free points + Eden doesn't die. Pairs well with instakill powers.
- Difficulty = Suicide Mode: **+50 SP** + immunity to precognition + free Cloak V2/V3 + 100% int/creative boost. **Best SP/dollar in the entire build.**
- Difficulty = Determination Mode: **+100 SP** + immunity to precognition + free Cloak V2/V3 + Contessa restricted + 2000% determination + **one wish on victory** (the wish alone is worth the whole build).
- Shard of the Thinker Entity: **+10 SP**
- Shard Ranking = Queen: **costs 20 CP** (unlocks Tier 1/2/3; NOT a gain)
- Shard = Mundane: **gains 60 CP but blocks ALL powers** (no shard powers; ambiguous on Shardless); only viable as a challenge run

### Power budget slots

- **Power Copy**: 1 pick, costs SP (3–12 SP across 50 capes)
- **Tier 3 Powers**: 5 picks
- **Tier 2 Powers**: 3 picks
- **Tier 1 Powers**: 1 pick
- **Combo Powers**: 5 picks (often require both Tier picks)
- **(Shardless) One Pointers**: 12 powers, 1 SP each (shardless path)
- **(Multi-Pointer) Shardless Powers**: 9 picks, 5–25 SP
