## The 'I win' immunity combo (corrected: PtV + All Seeing Precognition + Suicide/Det Mode + Cloak Mark II)

Per Suicide Mode: 'You are totally immune to precognition in any form other than your own.'
Per All Seeing Precognition: 'Your powers have no blindspots. They can see Eidolon, The Endbringers, The Entity, Mantellum, and all other blind spots. Actually, your power does have one blindspot... it cannot see those with the Cloak V2 perk.'
Per Cloak Mark II: 'You are a blind spot to human precogs, including those with All-Seeing Precognition.'

**Reading**: Suicide Mode immunity applies to *external* precog (Contessa, Dinah). Your own PtV still works because 'other than your own.' All Seeing Precognition removes PtV's blindspots (Eidolon, Entities, Endbringers, trigger events). Cloak Mark II hides you from All-Seeing Precognition users. So you have:

1. You are immune to all external precog (Contessa can't see you).
2. You have PtV with no blindspots (you can see everything, including Entities).
3. Cloak II/III free perks provide extra precog resistance (Cloak II blocks ALL human precogs, Cloak III blocks Endbringer/Entity precogs).
4. **Cloak Mark II (2 SP + 3 CP)** hides you from enemy All-Seeing Precognition users.
5. The wish on victory (Determination Mode) is the cherry on top.

**Total cost**: 50 SP (Suicide Mode) + 15 SP (PtV combo) + 20 SP + 10 CP (All Seeing Precognition) + 2 SP + 3 CP (Cloak Mark II) + free Cloak II/III perks = **87 SP + 13 CP + 1 wish.**

**This is the strongest single-build in the CYOA.** (v1's claim of 65 SP was incorrect — PtV alone has blindspots; you need All Seeing Precognition to remove them.)
