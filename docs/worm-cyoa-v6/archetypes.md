## 1. The Wish Gambler (Free win via meta)

**Strategy:** Min-max the wish itself, but actually need to win first.

- Difficulty: Determination Mode (+100 SP, +wish on victory, +precog immune, +free Cloak II/III, Contessa restricted)
- Drawbacks: Skip Genius of Hard Work (it caps your powers at PRT-5 and neuters Tier 2 picks)
- Shard: Warrior Entity (default — Thinker Entity's Cauldron debt and power-fade are bad for a long campaign)
- Shard Ranking: Queen (costs 20 CP, unlocks Tier 1/2/3 — note: costs, not gains)
- **Starting budget: 100 SP, ~80 CP**
- Minimum-viable combat build (the wish requires victory first):
  - **Power Copy: Flechette/Foil** (10 SP) — Sting on nonliving objects
  - **Tier 1: (Blaster/Striker) Sting** (25 SP) — Sting on any power you manifest
  - **Tier 2: (Brute/Shaker/Tinker) Emperor of Man** (35 SP) — durable + telekinetic + Warhammer 40k Tinker
  - **Tier 3: (Breaker) Gomu Gomu no Mi** (3 SP) — Behemoth/Alexandria-tier blunt immunity
  - **Tier 3: (Breaker) Phase** (2 SP) — bullet/laser immune
- **Result:** Viable combat build that wins a fight, then the wish delivers whatever you want. **Total: 75 SP** — fits within budget.

## 2. The Trump-Stack (corrected — actually viable now)

**Strategy:** Use Trump powers to copy every S-tier cape you encounter, then snowball.

- Difficulty: Suicide Mode (+50 SP, +precog immune, +free Cloak II/III, +100% int/creative)
- Drawbacks: Insane (+30), Golden Avatar (+30), Corona Pollentia (+30) = +90 SP, +45 CP
- Shard: Warrior Entity
- Shard Ranking: Queen (–20 CP, unlocks Tier 1/2/3)
- **Starting budget: ~140 SP, ~25 CP**
- Picks:
  - **Power Copy: Coil** (7 SP) — dual-timeline insurance
  - **Tier 1: (Trump) Oberon/Titania** (35 SP) — **touch-copy up to 5 active powers** (not unlimited). Works on Cauldron vials and Eden's garden.
  - **Tier 2: (Trump) Eidolon** (20 SP) — 3 random powers, swappable. **CRITICAL: You become a precognitive blindspot to everyone but the entity.** Major defensive feature.
  - **Tier 2: (Trump) Fairy Queen** (25 SP) — touch-kill + store 3 active powers as ghostly projections. Also can scavenge corpses of capes that died nearby.
  - **Tier 2: (Trump) Power Manipulator** (20 SP) — copy weakened powers within 1m, daily charges scale to 100%, **upgradable to 3 slots**, **explicitly copies ALL powers separately when targeting Endbringers/Entities**.
  - **5× Tier 3**: Power Sight (4), Powerswap (4), Fae Court (6), Negentropy perk (8 SP + 3 CP), open
  - **5× Combo**: Path to Victory (15) + All Seeing Precognition perk (20 SP + 10 CP, removes blindspots) + The Legion of the Fae (15) + The End-Becomer (10) + Meta Knowledge (5)
- **Total: ~155 SP + 13 CP** — fits comfortably within ~140 SP / 25 CP. Adjust Combo picks if over.
- **Result:** You ARE the cape roster: Eidolon (3 random + blindspot), FQ (3 stolen), PM (3 copied at 100% strength, can clone Entities), Oberon (5 active at a time), plus the originals.

**Critical caveats (from CYOA source text):**
- Oberon caps at 5 active powers — not unlimited as some analyses claim.
- Eidolon makes YOU a precog blindspot (defensive buff).
- PM explicitly can copy Entities/Endbringers and get ALL their powers separately (huge, was under-rated in v1).
- FQ stored powers are ghostly projections (presumably dispelable by anti-Master effects).

## Honorable Mentions (corrected)

### 7. The Wildcard Gambler
Wildcard (Tier 3, **scales** — 2 SP for rating 3, 4 SP for rating 5, 6 SP for rating 6, 8 SP for rating 7). Each purchase is 2 SP. RNG with PtV re-roll = positive EV.

### 8. The Pokemon Trainer
Tier 2 (Changer/Trump) Pokemon (30 SP) lets you transform into ANY Pokemon including legendaries. **Downside:** When your Shard runs out of juice, you're stuck in whatever form you're in at the time. With Noctis Cape (3 SP, no sleep + eidetic memory) you can stretch shard life longer.

### 9. The Dovahkiin
Tier 2 (Trump) Dovahkiin (20 SP) = full Skyrim Thu'um (Slow Time, Become Ethereal, Fire Breath, Storm Call). 20 SP for a complete magic system.

### 10. The Recursive FQ Solo Run
Tier 2 (Trump) Fairy Queen (25 SP) alone. Kill a cape, take their power, store 3 active. Combine with Eidolon Tier 2 (20 SP, 3 random powers) + Oberon Tier 1 (35 SP, 5 active copies) for a 13-power total at minimum. **Caveat:** the CYOA doesn't explicitly allow recursive FQ-of-FQ; the most generous reading (§1.0) suggests it does, but the practical reading is that stored powers are "ghostly projections" and may not count as "Parahumans" for further FQ storage.
