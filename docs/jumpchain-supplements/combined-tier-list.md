# Jumpchain Supplement Stack — Cross-Supplement Tier List

> **Purpose.** Single document ranking the strongest picks across all 5 supplements (Essential Body Mod, SB Body Mod, Personal Reality Supplement, Generic First Jump, Universal Drawbacks Supplement). Use this when deciding what to take first or where to spend limited budgets.
>
> **Read first.** Each per-supplement analysis is its own authoritative source. This document is a synthesis. Where per-supplement docs conflict (e.g. tier rankings), the per-supplement doc wins for supplement-specific builds; this doc wins for cross-supplement stacking decisions.

---

## §0. How to read this tier list

Every entry is tagged with its origin:
- **[EBM]** Essential Body Modification Supplement
- **[SBM]** SB Body Mod Supplement
- **[PRS]** Personal Reality Supplement
- **[GFJ]** Generic First Jump
- **[UDS]** Universal Drawbacks Supplement

Tiers:
- **S** = meta-defining or build-enabling. Take unless you have a hard reason not to.
- **A** = strong pick. Take in most builds.
- **B** = solid. Take if it fits your build theme.
- **C** = niche or overcosted. Skip unless it solves a specific problem.
- **D** = weak or trap. Skip unless for narrative reasons.

Each entry has a one-line justification. Where a perk has multiple tiers (e.g. "I to III"), the entry covers the perk as a whole with tier-specific notes.

## §4. The Body Mod philosophy split (EBMS vs SBBM)

Both supplements serve the same role (your Jumper's body baseline) but with fundamentally different design philosophies. Choose based on build theme.

### EBMS (Essential Body Modification Supplement) — "Stat-block" philosophy

**Best for:** Build-focused, numbers-driven builds. Long-chains where you want a known power curve.

- **Currency:** EP (Essence Points), not CP
- **Structure:** 4-dimensional Mode system (Starting / Essence / Advancement / EP Access) with massive combinatorial space
- **Perks:** 100+ named perks across Physical / Mental / Spiritual / Skill / Supernatural / Item / Companion categories, each with multiple tiers
- **Essences:** 18 selectable essences (Warlord, Scholar, Mad Doctor, Crafter, Assassin, Archmage, Brute, Superior, Lich, Vampire, Shapeshifter, King, Beast, Dragon, Explorer, Healer, Elemental, Druid)
- **Tier 3 Supernatural capstone:** Divinity. Godhood.
- **Philosophy:** Define your body like a character sheet. Each perk is a discrete ability with explicit costs.
- **Strengths:** Precision, granularity, build variety, the 18 Essences enable thematic builds.
- **Weaknesses:** Complexity overhead. The Mode system has many trap combinations. 100+ perks is a lot to read.

### SBBM (SB Body Mod Supplement) — "Comic-book" philosophy

**Best for:** Shapeshifter builds, body-as-canvas builds, training-focused builds.

- **Currency:** CP (1000 CP)
- **Structure:** Base-Form + Affinity + Augments + Powers
- **Base-Form:** Sex, Proportions, Hair, Color, Extra Bits, Anomalous (the visual)
- **Affinity:** Body / Mind / Heart (1 pick, gives discounts on 5 Augments)
- **Augments:** 15 stats × 3 levels = 45 tiered entries (Strength, Endurance, Speed, etc.)
- **Powers:** Shapeshifting / Variability / Permanence / Adaptability / Quality of Life / Gag
- **Philosophy:** Comic-book peak human as baseline. Build the body you imagine.
- **Strengths:** Clean structure, comic-book scaling, shapeshifting is a first-class citizen.
- **Weaknesses:** Less granular than EBMS. No Essences. 1000 CP ceiling may constrain optimization.

### Which to take?

**Take EBMS if:**
- You want to play a specific archetype (Warlord, Mage, Vampire, etc.)
- You want the Mode system for fine-tuned power curve
- You want the Divinity capstone
- You want a wider perk variety (100+ perks)

**Take SBBM if:**
- You want a shapeshifter build (Alt-Form Shift is free, Independent Forms and Coalescence are S-tier)
- You want clean comic-book scaling
- You want a faster build (less to read, easier to optimize)
- You want the "yuo" joke in your perk list

**Take BOTH if:** You want the "comic-book body + stat-block perks" combo. EBMS's Mode system and Essences + SBBM's clean Augment scaling. The supplements don't conflict; you can layer them. Most min-maxers take both.

## §5. S-tier cross-supplement synergies (picks that combine)

These combinations unlock the highest power levels. Listed in build order.

### Synergy 1: Retail Rocker + Gauntlet-Kun + Chain Drawback stack

**The math:**
- Retail Rocker doubles CP from all drawbacks
- Gauntlet-Kun doubles CP from all UDS drawbacks (but not Retail Rocker itself)
- Take 5+ Chain Drawbacks worth +500-1000 CP/jump base
- Both multipliers stack (multiplicatively for UDS drawbacks, additively with jump-native)

**Example:** Take Bitch-Chan (+200) + Random-Chan (+200) + Pseudo-Random-Chan (+50) + Thomas Wolfe Rule (+100) + Refresher Course (+100) + Old Home Week (+100) = +750 CP/jump base Chain Drawbacks. With Gauntlet-Kun doubling: +1500/jump. With Retail Rocker on top of that: +3000/jump. Over 10 jumps: +30,000 CP. Dominant.

**Caveat:** Gauntlet-Kun starts you at 0 CP, Retail Rocker forces full price. This is a hardcore mode build. See redteam.md for the risks.

### Synergy 2: UDS → Body Mod BP routing

**The mechanic:** "If you want, you can take a Chain Drawback to add half its value to the Body Mod Supplement... but doing so means you only get half its value in all jumps. Such a Chain Drawback cannot be put on Hiatus nor Revoked."

**Example:** Take Bitch-Chan (+200) and route half to Body Mod = +100 BP (one-time) + +100 CP/jump (half value forever). This is half of a drawback's CP value but you get a permanent Body Mod budget boost.

**Use case:** If you want to start your Body Mod with a bigger budget without taking Body Mod during the first jump. The trade is permanent half-value on the routed drawback.

### Synergy 3: UDS → PRS WP routing

**The mechanic:** "Warehouse Drawbacks do grant one-time boosts to the Warehouse if you make them Chain Drawbacks since they limit the utility of the Warehouse."

**Use case:** Warehouse Drawbacks from UDS (the 4 new in v1.13: New Warehouse Who's This, No Inventory, Timelapse, Threadripper) can be made Chain Drawbacks to grant one-time WP boosts to your PRS. This is a CP-to-WP conversion pipeline.

**Note:** Full Warehouse Drawback analysis requires the missing UDS categories (see uds-v1.13.txt source note). This synergy is based on the partial extraction.

### Synergy 4: EBM Blank + EBM Inertia of Self + EBM Corruption Resistance

**The trinity of protection.** Each covers a different threat vector:
- Blank = external observation (precog, scrying, mind reading)
- Inertia of Self = internal continuity (identity, memory, history)
- Corruption Resistance = soul integrity (possession, corruption, outsider influence)

**All three together:** You are unobservable, unchangeable, and uncorruptible. The only remaining threat is direct force, which you handle with Strength/Endurance/Resilience.

### Synergy 5: EBM Defense Piercing Techniques + SBM Resistance

**The "I hit you, you can't touch me" combo:**
- EBM DPT II: Pierce any defense below fiat
- SBM Resistance [300 CP]: Resists detrimental supernatural changes

Together: You can hit anything (no defense holds against you), and you can't be hit by supernatural effects (Resistance stops them). A completed offensive-defensive loop.

### Synergy 6: SBM Resolve [200] + EBM Mental Resistance III

**The mind-control double-tap:**
- SBM Resolve: Resists supernatural control attempts (2x investment needed to overpower, dispels at jump end)
- EBM Mental Resistance III: Flawless immunity to all mental intrusion

Together: Even if one fails (theoretical), the other catches it. Combined with Blank (no precog to target you with mind control in the first place) and Inertia (no one can alter you), this is a total mind-control immunity suite.

## §7. Power creep assessment of the full stack

The full stack (EBM + SBM + PRS + UDS + GFJ) represents a moderate-to-high power budget. Compared to a single-jump budget of ~1000 CP, the stack gives you access to:
- ~1000 CP starting in any jump (from UDS Chain Drawbacks)
- A fully-statted Body Mod (EBMS or SBM)
- A Personal Reality warehouse (PRS)
- Level-scaled perks (GFJ, if taken)
- Multiplier effects (Retail Rocker, Gauntlet-Kun, Affinity discounts)

**Verdict:** The stack is **strong but not invincible**. The "trinity of protection" (Blank + Inertia + Corruption Resistance) covers all observation/identity/soul threats. The "offense-defense loop" (DPT + Resistance) handles combat. But the stack does NOT cover:
- Conceptual attacks (the supplement authors didn't address these)
- Fate/luck manipulation (no perk explicitly counters this)
- True omnipotence (no perk grants this)

**Compared to Worm V6.1 v5 build:** The Jumpchain supplement stack is roughly equivalent in power to the Worm v5 "Golden Avatar + Gomu Gomu no PAUNCH" build — strong enough to solo most settings, not strong enough to solo the meta-threats (Scion, Endbringers). The supplements give you a Jumper baseline; the jump-specific perks are where you get setting-specific power.

**Compared to "Generic CYOA" power levels:** The stack is well above average. A Jumper with this stack is at "I can solo most non-cosmic settings comfortably" level.

**What this stack CANNOT do (as of v1.13/1.10):**
- Grant conceptual immunity (e.g., "I cannot be erased from existence")
- Grant true omnipotence
- Grant direct resistance to setting-specific cosmic-tier threats
- Guarantee you won't be wiped from continuity

**What it CAN do (most settings):**
- Survive any non-cosmic threat
- Beat any non-cosmic enemy
- Acquire any non-cosmic ability
- Build any non-cosmic power
- Resist any non-cosmic control
