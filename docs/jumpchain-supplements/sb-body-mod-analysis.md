### Design philosophy

The SB Body Mod (SBBM) treats the jumper's body as a persistent avatar across the chain. Two design choices shape every other rule.

1. **Comic-book peak human as the ceiling.** Augment level 3 reads, verbatim, "You perform on the level of a comic-book peak-human. Bench press up to two tons and shatter concrete or dent steel with your punches." That is the stat block the entire supplement is balanced around. A jumper with all 15 Augments at 200 CP is Batman-class, not Superman-class.
2. **Alt-Form first, stat-block second.** Where the Essential Body Mod Supplement (EBMS) is a flat list of body upgrades, SBBM spends an entire section teaching the reader how to build a custom species, then layers Shapeshifting, Variability, Permanence, and Coalescence powers on top. The supplement assumes you will collect Alt-Forms from other Jumps and want them to interoperate.

Two mechanical levers follow from this. First, every Augment applies across all Alt-Forms (Note: "Augments and Powers apply across all Alt-Forms, and CP invested in them will not be refunded for different Alt-Forms"). Second, the Affinity system plus the Ascension toggle form a discount ladder that can render an entire category of Augments free. Both are covered in depth below.

### What SBBM is not

It is not a power source. Nothing in the supplement gives you a parahuman ability, a spell list, a ki technique, or a magic system. SBBM makes you a better animal. Every supernatural effect has to come from a Jump document. This matters for cross-supplement planning: SBBM is a force multiplier on whatever else you bring, not a substitute for it.

## §2 Base-Form Section Analysis

Base-Form is the custom-species builder. Source text: "Each option for adjusting your Base-Form includes the benefits of the preceding, cheaper Levels for the price of only that Level." That line is doing a lot of work. It means paying 100 CP for Proportions also gives you the Free and 50 CP effects. It does **not** mean paying 100 CP for Proportions gives you 100 CP off elsewhere.

A second critical rule, verbatim: "Adjustments are single use purchases - after finishing this section you cannot re-adjust your Base-Form, unless you have specifically paid for the relevant options of the Variability power." Base-Form is a one-shot unless you buy Variable Form (300 CP, see §5).

A third rule, verbatim: "Any Perks, Skills, Powers or other improvements gained while in one of your Base-Forms apply to all Alt-Forms. Outside of the additional options regarding Base-Forms outlined in this document a Base-Form counts as regular an Alt-Form." Translation: Base-Form is just a special Alt-Form you designed yourself.

## §3 Affinity System Analysis

The Affinity system is the most important meta-mechanic in SBBM. One purchase shapes the discount profile of your entire build.

### The mechanic

Source text, verbatim: "If you wish, you may purchase a single Affinity for your body, granting you discounts on certain augments." Cost is 100 CP. Only one Affinity may be purchased.

Discount interaction with Augments (from the Augments section header): "Discounts make Augments free at the 50 CP level, and 50% off at higher levels. Stacking the discounts from an Affinity and forfeiting Ascension makes the affected Augments free at the 100 CP level and 75% off at the 200 CP level."

The full ladder:

| Configuration | 50 CP Augment | 100 CP Augment | 200 CP Augment |
|---------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| No Affinity, Ascension taken | 50 CP | 100 CP | 200 CP |
| No Affinity, Ascension forfeited | 50 CP | 100 CP | 200 CP (no discount from forfeit alone) |
| Affinity, Ascension taken | Free | 50 CP | 100 CP |
| Affinity, Ascension forfeited | Free | Free | 50 CP |

Forfeiting Ascension without an Affinity grants no discount at all. This is a common misreading. The Note explicitly says: "Choosing not to take this power grants a discount on all Augments." But the Augments header restricts the stacking benefit to the Affinity + forfeit combo. Read together: forfeiting Ascension alone does nothing. It only matters when paired with an Affinity.

### The three Affinities

**Body [100 CP]** — discounts Strength (primary), Endurance, Speed, Resilience, Reflex. The flavor text is written in a parody of a drill sergeant, with the intentional typo "yuo" (confirmed in Notes: "The 'yuo' in the body affinity is entirely intentional."). Recommended for combat builds, Brute archetypes, and any jumper who expects to take physical damage. The discount profile makes the classic "comic-book peak human" stat block trivially achievable.

**Mind [100 CP]** — discounts Logic, Memory, Resolve, Coordination, Perception. Recommended for Thinker archetypes, scholars, investigators, and any build that leans on information warfare. Note that Resolve 200 CP under Mind Affinity + forfeit Ascension is free, and Resolve 200 is one of the strongest Augments in the supplement (see §4).

**Heart [100 CP]** — discounts Charisma, Appeal, Empathy, Flexibility, Fertility. Recommended for social builds, diplomat archetypes, legacy dynasties, and any jumper whose primary tool is other people. Often undervalued because the Augments look soft; Fertility 200 CP (offspring inherit your Augments) is a generational-scale min-max lever.

### The "yuo" joke

In the Body Affinity description, the source text reads: "but why the hell would yuo want to call yourself any kind of artist?" The "yuo" is deliberate. The Notes section ends with: "The 'yuo' in the body affinity is entirely intentional." It is a community in-joke, likely mocking a specific poster's typo. Mechanically irrelevant. Worth knowing only because players who quote the Body Affinity text tend to preserve the typo as a shibboleth.

### Recommended Affinity by build type

| Build type | Affinity | Why |
|------------|----------|-----|
| Brute / street-level hero | Body | Strength 200 + Resilience 200 both discounted; reach the 2-ton ceiling cheaply. |
| Speedster | Body | Speed 200 discounted; pair with Reflex for the dodge layer. |
| Thinker / investigator | Mind | Logic 200 + Memory 200 + Resolve 200 all discounted; the discount on Resolve 200 alone is worth the Affinity. |
| Social / diplomat | Heart | Charisma 200 + Empathy 200 discounted; together they make you a near-perfect reader and speaker. |
| Legacy / dynastic | Heart | Fertility 200 discounted; pass Augments to offspring. |
| Escape artist / infiltrator | Mind or Heart | Coordination 200 (Mind) or Flexibility 200 (Heart); both are escape utility. |
| Generalist | Mind | Broadest set of useful Augments, and the discount stack hits the expensive picks hardest. |

## §4 Augment Tier List (45 entries)

Tiering methodology:
- S = take-always at full price; fundamentally changes what kind of character you are.
- A = strong picks worth budgeting for; would take at full price if needed.
- B = solid picks; take if discounted or if your build leans that way.
- C = situational or redundant with real-life capability (Note: "you may take any level of an Augment you are able to match in real life for free").
- D = luxury or marginal; take only if CP is leftover.

The Note about real-life capability matters: a jumper who is in real life a trained strongman can take Strength 50 CP for free. Buying Strength 100 CP still costs the full 100 CP. The free-tier clause makes most 50 CP Augments C or D for self-inserts.

## §5 Powers Tier List

Powers do not stack with each other unless stated. Source text: "Options in this Section have to be bought separately and do not grant access to the functionality of any other listed option. Powers are bought independently from Base-Forms."

Two free powers are mutually exclusive (Notes: "You can only pick one of the following Perks: Alt-Form Shift or Singular Form"). This is the single most important constraint in the Powers section.

## §7 Build Archetypes

Five named archetypes spanning the design space. Each is built to fit the 1000 CP budget.

### 1. Peak Human Batman

The street-level hero. No shapeshifting, no flashy powers. Just the comic-book peak-human stat block and the QoL layer to support it.

- **Affinity:** Body, Ascension forfeited (cheapest path to all-200 physical Augments).
- **Powers:** Singular Form (the locked-avatar flavor), Agelessness, Resistance.

## §9 Edge Cases & Rulings

### Ascension + training modifiers

Source text: "I won't try to balance Ascension interacting with training modifiers from other documents, just do what feels right in that regard."

The author explicitly punts on this. Community rulings vary. The most common interpretation is that Jump-doc training multipliers stack with Ascension's training rate but do not change the fiat-backed ceiling (you can train Augments faster, but cannot train past 200 CP level through Ascension alone). The Notes also specify: "training a single augment from zero to its maximum level to take roughly 50 Years, initial gains coming faster."

A Solid Foundation (free, requires 500 CP in Augments) doubles training effectiveness. This is the only in-supplement training modifier and the cleanest ruling baseline: A Solid Foundation halves the 50-year estimate to 25 years per Augment. Out-of-supplement modifiers are at GM discretion.

### Singular Form vs Alt-Form Shift mutual exclusion

Notes: "You can only pick one of the following Perks: Alt-Form Shift or Singular Form."

Singular Form text: "Note that this prevents you from changing forms at all until you pick up a Perk, spell or ability explicitly allowing you to do so somewhere. Taking this power precludes you from taking any Shapeshifting or Variability powers from this document."

The exclusion is wider than the Note suggests. Singular Form locks you out of the entire Shapeshifting and Variability sections, not just Alt-Form Shift. Pick Singular Form only if you want a fixed avatar or are houseruling that transformation requires explicit permission.

### Coalescence contradictions

Notes: "When merging Alt-Forms with contradicting strengths and weaknesses using Coalescence you may choose which applies."

You pick which version of a contradicting trait is active. This is per-contradiction, not per-form. If Form A is immune to fire and Form B is weak to fire, you choose which applies for the duration of the Coalescence. You cannot flip mid-combat unless your GM rules otherwise.

The bigger ambiguity: Coalescence says "you also count as being in every single one of them, should it become relevant." This is unbounded. If one Alt-Form is vulnerable to sunlight and another is not, you choose which applies. If one is a vampire (undead) and another is a living human, do you count as both? The supplement does not say. The most permissive reading is yes; the strictest reading is that you pick one physiological state at a time. Flag this for your table.

### Augments and Powers across Alt-Forms

Notes: "Augments and Powers apply across all Alt-Forms, and CP invested in them will not be refunded for different Alt-Forms. Only the CP invested in the Base-Form part of the document are freed up for additional Forms, and only may be spent on options from that same section."

Translation: When you buy Additional Base-Form, you get a fresh 1000 CP-equivalent budget for the Base-Form section only. You cannot use it to buy more Augments or Powers. Augments and Powers are chain-wide; they apply to every form you ever take.

### Ascension limits

Notes: "Ascension does not allow you to manipulate your Base-Form directly (such as gaining additional Extra Bits), or attain extra Powers (such as obtaining agelessness)."

Ascension improves existing Augments through training. It does not grant new Extra Bits, new Powers, or new capabilities outside the Augment list.

### Resolve limits

Notes: "Resolve does not allow you to overpower e.g. outright mind control or psychic puppeteering, and also can't protect you from getting your brain chemistry interfered with by outside sources, those are cases where only the increased resistance applies."

Resolve 200 reduces the cost to apply supernatural influence to roughly 2x. It does not make you immune. Direct mind control still works if the attacker pays the doubled price. Brain-chemistry interference (drugs, neurohackers) gets only the resistance modifier, not the dispel-on-jump-end clause.

### The self-assessment rule

Notes: "Seeing as the fiction behind Jumpchain (usually) is that it is you (the real life person) that is going on the Jumpchain adventure you may take any level of an Augment you are able to match in real life for free. Buying a better level will still cost the full amount of CP in this case, however."

The jumper's real-life capabilities translate to free Augment levels. A real-life Olympic weightlifter takes Strength 50 CP free. A real-life polymath takes Logic 50 CP and Memory 50 CP free. Buying the next tier up still costs full CP; there is no partial credit for already meeting the lower tier.

### Anomalous per Alt-Form

Notes: "It is permissible to take differing levels of Anomalous for each Alt-Form."

Each Alt-Form has its own Anomalous level. A jumper can have a +0 Base-Form for everyday use and a +300 CP Anomalous combat Alt-Form. The CP from each Anomalous purchase is spent only within that form's Base-Form budget.

### Variable Form scope

Source text: "Note that this only makes the Base-Forms you possess variable and doesn't let you create new, alternative Base-Forms. Also note that you only may redistribute the CP invested in the Base-Form section of this document, not those invested in any other section."

Variable Form reshuffles existing Base-Form CP. It does not create new Base-Forms (use Additional Base-Form for that) and does not touch Augment or Powers CP. The Potential Extra Bit exists to hold unspent CP within the Base-Form pool during reshuffles.

### Ship Of Theseus interpretation

Notes: "Ship Of Theseus: As long as it maintains the function of the perk you may use any 'in Jump Lore' you can come up with, e.g. replacing your 'Brain' with some sort of Phylactery you have to carry with you in your body etc."

The "brain" in the perk text is a functional slot, not a literal organ. A phylactery, a soul gem, a backup disc, any in-Jump Lore-compatible substitute works as long as it carries the jumper's continuity.

### Singular Form and post-Jump default

Notes: "Singular Form is intended for those that houserule a Jumper needs a transformation perk, or more generally a piece of rules text explicitly permitting it, to change between Alt-Forms. It does not prevent you from gaining Alt-Forms. If you wish you may decide for it to make you default to an Alt-Form different from your Base-Form between Jumps."

Singular Form does not block acquisition of new Alt-Forms. It blocks unsanctioned shifting. A house rule is needed to default to a non-Base-Form between Jumps.

## §10 Power Creep Assessment

### SBBM vs EBMS (Essential Body Mod Supplement)

The two supplements cover overlapping ground with divergent design philosophies. Both are community-standard body mods for Jumpchain. Both cost 1000 CP. Both grant Augments and Powers. The differences are in framing.

| Axis | SBBM v1.2 | EBMS |
|------|-----------|------|
| Ceiling | Comic-book peak human (Batman-tier) | Higher stat ceiling, scales into superhuman |
| Format | Tiered Augments (50/100/200) with Affinity discounts | Flatter perk list, fewer multi-tier chains |
| Shapeshifting | First-class citizen. Dedicated Base-Form section, Alt-Form Shift free, Variability powers. | Secondary. Often bolted on as a perk rather than built into the chassis. |
| Meta-mechanic | Affinity system shapes the whole build | Stat-block and perk list, no equivalent meta-lever |
| Long-term scaling | Ascension: train Augments to 200 CP over ~50 years | Variable by version; often has its own training path |
| Tone | Comic-book logic, Required Secondary Powers explicitly invoked | More literal, more stat-block oriented |

### Where SBBM is stronger

- **Shapeshifter builds.** SBBM is the better supplement if your chain collects Alt-Forms. Alt-Form Tuning alone makes every future Jump's form-gain better.
- **Discount-stacked min-max.** Affinity + forfeit Ascension renders entire Augment categories free. EBMS has no equivalent lever.
- **Long-term scaling.** Ascension is fiat-backed improvement that carries into Gauntlets and Alt-Form-restricted Jumps. This is rare in the supplement ecosystem.
- **Comic-book logic.** The Required Secondary Powers clause on Extra Bits is explicit. Wings come with the muscle and nervous system needed to use them. Less fanwanking required.

### Where EBMS is stronger

- **Raw stat ceiling.** SBBM caps at 2-ton bench press and 250 km/h. EBMS often allows higher peaks, depending on version.
- **Flat perk list.** Easier to budget when every option is one tier. SBBM's three-tier structure invites over-investment in single Augments.
- **Less bookkeeping.** SBBM's Affinity, Anomalous, Ascension, Base-Form CP pool, and Alt-Form Modifier rules are a lot to track. EBMS is more pick-and-play.

### Power creep in SBBM itself

Comparing v1.0 (inferred), v1.1, and v1.2:

- **v1.1 added:** Limb Conversion, Mimicry, Reflex, Resolve, Empathy, the Powers-section rework, the rename of Inhuman to Anomalous. This was the major expansion. Reflex/Resolve/Empathy filled gaps in the Augment list and brought the tier count to 15.
- **v1.2 added:** Gills upgrade, Potential, clarification on Affinity + Ascension stacking, expanded Agelessness functionality, Need Not. Mostly polish and clarification, with two minor additions (Potential, Need Not).

v1.2 is more cleanup than power creep. The balance levers (Affinity discount, Ascension training time, Singular Form exclusion) are unchanged. The supplement's ceiling has not moved.

The single most impactful v1.2 change is the explicit Affinity + forfeit Ascension stacking rule. Pre-v1.2, tables argued over whether the two discounts combined. v1.2 settled it: they do. This makes the discount-stacked builds in §7 and §8 RAW rather than table ruling.

## §11 v1.1 to v1.2 Changelog Impact

Source changelog, verbatim:

- More spelling corrections
- Slightly cheaper wings
- Added Clarifications to the 'Limb Conversion' option
- Added upgrade option for gills
- Added 'Potential' under Bits to make it completely RAW that a Jumper using Variable Form can 'store' CP they currently don't need for their form.
- Clarified stacking Affinities with the discount from forfeiting Ascension.
- Slightly nerfed Speed III
- Expanded functionality of 'Ageless'
- Reworded Waste Not
- Added 'Need Not' to Quality Of Life section

### What matters for min-max

**High impact:**

1. **Affinity + Ascension stacking clarification.** The biggest ruling in the changelog. Confirms that the two discounts combine. This is the foundation of the Stat Ceiling, Mind Palace, and Heart Of The Party combos in §6. Pre-v1.2 builds that assumed this combination were operating on a table ruling. Post-v1.2 they are RAW.

2. **Speed III nerf.** The maneuverability caveat ("at such speeds you will require significant space to be able to properly maneuver") was added or reinforced in v1.2. This drops Speed 200 from S to A in the tier list. Indoor combat and tight streets blunt the top speed. Builds that relied on Speed 200 for close-quarters work need re-evaluation.

3. **Agelessness expansion.** v1.2 added the apparent-age-independent-of-biological-age clause. Minor for combat builds, significant for social and infiltration builds that want to look 25 while being biologically 40, or vice versa.

**Medium impact:**

4. **Potential added.** Codifies the CP-storage pattern that Variable Form users were already running. Removes a table argument. No build-changing effect; just RAW clarity.

5. **Gills upgrade.** Adds the "breathe underwater through unspecified means" 50 CP upgrade to the existing 100 CP gills. Also confirms the free upgrade if Mimicry was purchased. Relevant for aquatic-build planning.

6. **Wings cost reduction.** "Slightly cheaper wings" is vague in the changelog. The current text has Wings at 100 CP for gliding and 150 CP total for powered flight. The delta is small but matters for tight Base-Form budgets.

**Low impact:**

7. **Limb Conversion clarification.** Cleaned up the rule about unused discounts expiring. No mechanical change, just clearer text.

8. **Waste Not rewording.** Cosmetic. The mechanical effect (digestive system fully processes waste) is unchanged.

9. **Need Not added.** New Quality of Life perk. Overindulging has no negative effects, but does not include resistance to alcohol or poisons. Pure QoL.

10. **Spelling corrections.** No mechanical impact.

### Builds that change between v1.1 and v1.2

- Any build using the Affinity + Ascension discount stack is now RAW rather than table-dependent. This is most combat and Thinker builds.
- Any build using Speed 200 for close-quarters work needs to account for the maneuverability nerf. Open-field builds are unaffected.
- Any build with Agelessness can now fine-tune apparent age. Cosmetic but useful.
- Any Variable Form build can now use Potential to store unspent CP without a table argument.

### Builds that do not change

- Pure Permanence builds (Singular Form + Agelessness + Regeneration + Resistance) are unaffected.
- Pure QoL builds are unaffected except for the addition of Need Not.
- Builds that do not use Affinity or Ascension are unaffected.
